Each of the following properties/notions makes sense for an arbitrary metric spaces $X$. Which are topological (i.e. dependent only on the collection of open subsets of $X$ and not on the metric generating the open subsets)? Justify your answers.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] boundedness of a subset of $X$.
\item[(ii)] closed-ness of a subset of $X$.
\item[(iii)] notion that a subset of $X$ is closed \emph{and} bounded.
\item[(iv)] total boundedness of $X$.
\item[(v)] completeness of $X$.
\item[(vi)] notion that $X$ is complete \emph{and} totally bounded.
\end{enumerate}
