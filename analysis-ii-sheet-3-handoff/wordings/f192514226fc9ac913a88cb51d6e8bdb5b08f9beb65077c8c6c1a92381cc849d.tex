Define $f:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$ by $f(x,y,z)=(e^{x+y+z},\cos{x^2}y)$. Without making use of partial derivatives, show that $f$ is everywhere differentiable and find $Df(a)$ at each $a\in\mathbb R^3$. Find all partial derivatives of $f$ and hence, using appropriate results on partial derivatives, give an alternative proof of this result.
