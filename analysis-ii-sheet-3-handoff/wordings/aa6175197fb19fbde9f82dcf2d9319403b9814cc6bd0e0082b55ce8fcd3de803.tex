Consider the map $f:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ given by $f(x)=x/\|x\|$ for $x\ne0$, and $f(0)=0$. Show that $f$ is differentiable except at $0$, and that
\[
Df(x)(h)=\frac{h}{\|x\|}-\frac{x(x\cdot h)}{\|x\|^3}.
\]
Verify that $Df(x)(h)$ is orthogonal to $x$ and explain geometrically why this is the case.
