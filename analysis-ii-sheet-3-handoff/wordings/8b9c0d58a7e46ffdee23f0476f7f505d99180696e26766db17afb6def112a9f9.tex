Let $U\subset\mathbb R^n$ be open, $f:U\to\mathbb R$ and $a\in U$. A differentiable curve passing through $a$ is a differentiable map $\gamma:(-1,1)\to\mathbb R^n$ with $\gamma(0)=a$. If $f\circ\gamma$ is differentiable at $0$ for every differentiable curve $\gamma$ passing through $a$, does it follow that $f$ is differentiable at $a$?
