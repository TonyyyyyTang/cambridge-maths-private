## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
By constructing a suitable Green’s function, find the solution to the initial value problem
\[
\left\{\begin{aligned}
y^{(n)}&=f,\quad t>0\\
y(0)=\cdots=y^{(n-1)}(0)&=0.
\end{aligned}\right.
\]
Hence prove Taylor’s theorem with integral remainder for a $n$-times continuously differentiable functions.
```
