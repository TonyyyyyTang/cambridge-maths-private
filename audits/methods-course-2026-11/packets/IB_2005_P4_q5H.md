## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Show how the general solution of the wave equation for $y(x,t)$,
\[
\frac1{c^2}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial t^2}y(x,t)-\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}y(x,t)=0,
\]
can be expressed as
\[
y(x,t)=f(ct-x)+g(ct+x).
\]
Show that the boundary conditions $y(0,t)=y(L,t)=0$ relate the functions $f$ and $g$ and require them to be periodic with period $2L$.

Show that, with these boundary conditions,
\[
\frac12\int_0^L\biggl(\frac1{c^2}\Bigl(\frac{\partial y}{\partial t}\Bigr)^2+\Bigl(\frac{\partial y}{\partial x}\Bigr)^2\biggr)\,\mathrm dx=\int_{-L}^{L}g'(ct+x)^2\,\mathrm dx,
\]
and that this is a constant independent of $t$.
```
