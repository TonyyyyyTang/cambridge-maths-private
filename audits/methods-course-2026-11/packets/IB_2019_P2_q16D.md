## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
For $n=0,1,2,\ldots$, the degree $n$ polynomial $C_n^\alpha(x)$ satisfies the differential equation
\[
(1-x^2)y''-(2\alpha+1)xy'+n(n+2\alpha)y=0
\]
where $\alpha$ is a real, positive parameter. Show that, when $m\neq n$,
\[
\int_a^b C_m^\alpha(x)\,C_n^\alpha(x)\,w(x)\,dx=0
\]
for a weight function $w(x)$ and values $a<b$ that you should determine.

Suppose that the roots of $C_n^\alpha(x)$ that lie inside the domain $(a,b)$ are $\{x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_k\}$, with $k\leq n$. By considering the integral
\[
\int_a^b C_n^\alpha(x)\prod_{i=1}^k(x-x_i)\,w(x)\,dx,
\]
show that in fact all $n$ roots of $C_n^\alpha(x)$ lie in $(a,b)$.
```
