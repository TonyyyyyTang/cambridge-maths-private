## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Symmetric tensors.} (i) For any tensor $T_{ik}$ in $R^3$, prove directly from the transformation property of a tensor that the quantities
\[
\alpha=T_{ii},\quad \beta=T_{ik}T_{ki},\quad \gamma=T_{ik}\,T_{km}\,T_{mi}
\]
are invariant under rotation of the coordinate axes.

(ii) If $T_{ik}$ is a symmetric tensor, express these invariants in terms of its eigenvalues. Deduce that the cubic equation for the eigenvalues $\lambda$ is
\[
\lambda^3-\alpha\lambda^2+\tfrac12(\alpha^2-\beta)\lambda-\tfrac16(\alpha^3-3\alpha\beta+2\gamma)=0.
\]
```
