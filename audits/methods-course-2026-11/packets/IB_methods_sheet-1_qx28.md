## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Recasting in Sturm-Liouville form}. Express the following equations in Sturm-Liouville form:
\[
\text{(i)}\quad(1-x^2)y''-2xy'+n(n+1)y=0,\qquad
\text{(ii)}\quad x(x-1)y''+[(1+a+b)x-c]y'+aby=0,
\]
where $n,a,b$, and $c$ are constants.

(iii) Find the eigenvalues and eigenfunctions for
\[
y''+4y'+(4+\lambda)y=0,\quad y(0)=y(1)=0.
\]
What is the orthogonality relation for these eigenfunctions?
```
