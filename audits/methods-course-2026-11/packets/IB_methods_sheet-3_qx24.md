## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
By using differentiation and shift properties, calculate the Fourier transform of the Gaussian distribution $f(x)=\exp[-n^2(x-\mu)^2]$ for constants $n$ and $\mu$.

Now let $\mu=0$, and consider $\delta_n(x)=(n/\sqrt\pi)f(x)$. Sketch $\delta_n(x)$ and $\widetilde\delta_n(k)$ for small and large $n$. Evaluate
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\delta_n(x)\,dx.
\]
What is happening as $n\to\infty$?
```
