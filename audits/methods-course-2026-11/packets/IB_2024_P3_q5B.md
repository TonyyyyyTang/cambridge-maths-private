## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $u(r,\theta)$ satisfy the Laplace equation
\[
\nabla^2u\equiv\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial r^2}+\frac1r\frac{\partial u}{\partial r}+\frac1{r^2}\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial\theta^2}=0
\]
in the annulus $\mathcal A$ given in polar coordinates by
\[
\mathcal A=\{(r,\theta):a<r<b,0\le\theta<2\pi\}.
\]
Use separation of variables to derive a general expression for $u$. Given boundary conditions $u(a,\theta)=0$ and $u(b,\theta)=\cos 2\theta$, find $u$ explicitly.
```
