## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
The Legendre polynomial $P_n(x)$ satisfies
\[
(1-x^2)P_n''-2xP_n'+n(n+1)P_n=0,\quad n=0,1,\ldots,\ -1\leqslant x\leqslant1.
\]
Show that $R_n(x)=P_n'(x)$ obeys an equation which can be recast in Sturm–Liouville form and has the eigenvalue $(n-1)(n+2)$. What is the orthogonality relation for $R_n(x),R_m(x)$ for $n\ne m$?
```
