## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Damped string motion.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] A uniform stretched string of length $\ell$, density per unit length $\mu$ and tension $T=\mu c^2$ is fixed at both ends. The motion of the string is resisted by the surrounding medium, the resistive force on unit length being $-2k\mu y_t$, where $y$ is the transverse displacement and the constant $k=\pi c/\ell$. Show that the equation of motion of the string is
\[
c^2\frac{\partial^2y}{\partial x^2}=\frac{\partial^2y}{\partial t^2}+2k\frac{\partial y}{\partial t},
\]
and find $y(x,t)$ given that $y(x,0)=A\sin(\pi x/\ell)$ and $y_t(x,0)=0$.
\item[(ii)] If an extra transverse force $F_0\sin(\pi x/\ell)\cos(\pi ct/\ell)$ per unit length acts on the string, find the resulting forced oscillation.
\end{enumerate}
```
