## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Let $f(x)$ be a $2\pi$-periodic function (i.e. $f(x)=f(x+2\pi)$ for all $x$) defined on $[-\pi,\pi]$ by
\[
f(x)=\begin{cases}x&x\in[0,\pi]\\-x&x\in[-\pi,0]\end{cases}
\]
Find the Fourier series of $f(x)$ in the form
\[
f(x)=\tfrac12 a_0+\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n\cos(nx)+\sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n\sin(nx).
\]
\item[(b)] Find the general solution to
\[
y''+2y'+y=f(x)
\]
where $f(x)$ is as given in part (a) and $y(x)$ is $2\pi$-periodic.
\end{enumerate}
```
