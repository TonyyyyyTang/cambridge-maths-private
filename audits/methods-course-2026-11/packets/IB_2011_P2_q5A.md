## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
The Legendre equation is
\[
(1-x^2)\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-2x\frac{dy}{dx}+n(n+1)y=0
\]
for $-1\leqslant x\leqslant1$ and non-negative integers $n$.

Write the Legendre equation as an eigenvalue equation for an operator $L$ in Sturm-Liouville form. Show that $L$ is self-adjoint and find the orthogonality relation between the eigenfunctions.
```
