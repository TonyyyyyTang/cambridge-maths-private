## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
By using separation of variables, solve Laplace’s equation
\[
\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}=0\qquad 0<x<1,\quad 0<y<1,
\]
subject to
\[
\begin{aligned}
u(0,y)&=0 && 0\leq y\leq1,\\
u(1,y)&=0 && 0\leq y\leq1,\\
u(x,0)&=0 && 0\leq x\leq1,\\
u(x,1)&=2\sin(3\pi x) && 0\leq x\leq1.
\end{aligned}
\]
```
