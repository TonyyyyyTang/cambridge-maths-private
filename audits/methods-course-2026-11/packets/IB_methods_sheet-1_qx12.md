## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Modes on a string.} A uniform string of line density $\mu$ and tension $T$ undergoes small transverse vibrations of amplitude $y(x,t)$. The string is fixed at $x=0$ and $x=\ell$, and satisfies the initial conditions
\[
y(x,0)=0,\qquad y_t(x,0)=\frac{4V}{\ell^2}x(\ell-x),\quad\text{for }0<x<\ell,
\]
where $y_t\equiv\partial y/\partial t$. Find the amplitudes of the normal modes and deduce the kinetic and potential energies of the string at time $t$. By comparison with the initial energy of the string show that
\[
\sum_{\text{n odd}}\frac1{n^6}=\frac{\pi^6}{960}.
\]
```
