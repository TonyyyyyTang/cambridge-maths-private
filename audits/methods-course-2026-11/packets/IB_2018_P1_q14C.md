## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Define the \emph{convolution} $f*g$ of two functions $f$ and $g$. Defining the \emph{Fourier transform} $\tilde f$ of $f$ by
\[\tilde f(k)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-ikx}f(x)\,dx,\]
show that
\[\widetilde{f*g}\,(k)=\tilde f(k)\,\tilde g(k).\]

Given that the Fourier transform of $f(x)=1/x$ is
\[\tilde f(k)=-i\pi\operatorname{sgn}(k),\]
find the Fourier transform of $\sin(x)/x^2$.
```
