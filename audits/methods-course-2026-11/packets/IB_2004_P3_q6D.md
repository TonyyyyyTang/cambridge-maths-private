## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let
\[
S[x]=\int_0^T\tfrac12\bigl(\dot x^2-\omega^2x^2\bigr)\,\mathrm{d}t,\qquad x(0)=a,\quad x(T)=b.
\]
For any variation $\delta x(t)$ with $\delta x(0)=\delta x(T)=0$, show that $\delta S=0$ when $x=x_c$ with
\[
x_c(t)=\frac1{\sin\omega T}\Bigl[a\sin\omega(T-t)+b\sin\omega t\Bigr].
\]

By using integration by parts, show that
\[
S[x_c]=\big[\tfrac12 x_c\dot x_c\big]_0^T=\frac{\omega}{2\sin\omega T}\Bigl[(a^2+b^2)\cos\omega T-2ab\Bigr].
\]
```
