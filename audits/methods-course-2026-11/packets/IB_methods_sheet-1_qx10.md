## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Fourier coefficients (full- and half-range series).} \begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] For the function $f(x)=(x^2-1)^2$ on the interval $-1\le x<1$, show that it has the Fourier series
\[
f(x)=\frac8{15}+\frac{48}{\pi^4}\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n^4}\cos n\pi x.
\]
Sketch the function $f(x)$ and comment on its differentiability and the convergence rate of its Fourier series.
\item[(ii)] Suppose that $f(x)=x^2$ for $0\le x\le\pi$. Express $f(x)$ as (a) a Fourier sine series, and (b) a cosine series, each having period $2\pi$. Sketch the functions represented by (a) and (b) in the range $-6\pi$ to $6\pi$. If the series (a) and (b) are differentiated term-by-term, how are the answers related (if at all) to the Fourier series for $g(x)=x$ and $h(x)=|x|$ each in the range $(-\pi,\pi)$?
\end{enumerate}
```
