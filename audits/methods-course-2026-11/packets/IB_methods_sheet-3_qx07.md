## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Brachistochrone.} The new London mayor, devoted to schemes for energy saving, wishes to design a fuel-less transport system driven by gravity. The proposal is that carriages should travel in frictionless underground tunnels, being released from rest at their point of departure A (Waterloo) and then allowed to run freely until arriving at their destination B (Paddington). Assuming gravity is uniform, show that the minimum travel time between two points at the same level and a distance $l$ apart is $(2\pi l/g)^{\frac12}$. [Note that the tunnels are then vertical at A and B which should make for an interesting tube ride.]
```
