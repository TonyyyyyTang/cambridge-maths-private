## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Show that whenever $\mathbf r\ne\mathbf r'$, the function $1/|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|$ satisfies Laplace’s equation. Find the potential inside a spherical region bounded by two (conducting) hemispheres upon which the potential takes the values $\pm V$ respectively. [\emph{Hint:} Recall the orthogonality relation $\int_{-1}^1P_\ell(x)P_m(x)\,dx=\frac{2}{2\ell+1}\delta_{\ell m}$.]
```
