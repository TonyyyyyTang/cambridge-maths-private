## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Finite asymptotics.} A linear differential operator is defined by
\[
L_xy=-\frac{1}{x^2}\frac{d}{dx}\left(x^2\frac{dy}{dx}\right)+y.
\]
By writing $y=z/x$ or otherwise, find those solutions of $L_xy=0$ which are either (a) bounded as $x\to0$, or (b) bounded as $x\to\infty$. Find the Green’s function $G(x,a)$ satisfying
\[
L_xG(x,a)=\delta(x-a),
\]
and both conditions (a) and (b). Use $G(x,a)$ to solve (subject to conditions (a) and (b))
\[
L_xy(x)=\begin{cases}1,&\text{for }0\le x\le R,\\0,&\text{for }x>R.\end{cases}
\]
Show that the solution has the form, for suitable constants $A,B$
\[
y(x)=\begin{cases}1+Ax^{-1}\sinh x,&\text{for }0\le x\le R,\\Bx^{-1}e^{-x},&\text{for }x>R.\end{cases}
\]
```
