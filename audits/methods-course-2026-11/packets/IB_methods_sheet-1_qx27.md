## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Laplace’s equation.} Show that the inverse fourier transform of the function
\[
\tilde f(k)=\begin{cases}
e^k-e^{-k},&|k|\le1,\\
0&|k|>1,
\end{cases}
\]
is
\[
f(x)=\frac{2i}{\pi(1+x^2)}(\cosh 1\sin x-x\cos x\sinh 1).
\]
Determine, by using fourier transforms, the solution of Laplace’s equation in the infinite strip $0\le y\le1$, i.e.
\[
\nabla^2\psi=0;\quad-\infty<x<\infty,\quad 0<y<1,
\]
where $\psi(x,0)=f(x)$ the function given above, and $\psi(x,1)=0$ for $-\infty<x<\infty$.

(This was a long tripos question (2004/4/II/15A) for Complex Methods on material now in the Methods schedule.)
```
