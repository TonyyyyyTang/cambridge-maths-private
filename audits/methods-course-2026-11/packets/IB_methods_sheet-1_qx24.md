## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Functions defined on $|x|<c$.} Calculate the fourier transforms of the following functions. All are non-zero only on the interval $|x|<c$, and zero elsewhere.
\[
\begin{gathered}
f(x)=1.\\
f(x)=e^{iax}.\\
f(x)=\sin(ax).\\
f(x)=\cos(ax).
\end{gathered}
\]
```
