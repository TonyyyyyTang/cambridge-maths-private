## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Delta function properties.} The function $\phi(x)$ is monotone increasing in $[a,b]$ and has a zero at $x=c$ where $a<c<b$. Show that
\[
\int_a^b f(x)\delta[\phi(x)]dx=\frac{f(c)}{|\phi'(c)|}.
\]
Show that the same formula applies if $\phi(x)$ is monotone decreasing and hence derive a formula for general $\phi(x)$ provided the zeros are simple. Also establish that
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|x|\delta(x^2-a^2)dx=1.
\]
```
