## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
The displacement $y=y(x,t)$ of a uniform string stretched between $x=0$ and $x=L$ satisfies the wave equation with the boundary conditions $y(0,t)=y(L,t)=0$. For $t<0$ the string oscillates in the fundamental mode $y(x,t)=A\sin(\pi x/L)\sin(\pi ct/L)$. A musician strikes the midpoint of the string impulsively at time $t=0$ so that the change in $\partial y/\partial t$ at $t=0$ is $\lambda\delta(x-\frac12 L)$. Find $y=y(x,t)$ for $t>0$.
```
