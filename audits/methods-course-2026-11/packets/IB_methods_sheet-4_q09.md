## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Dirichlet Green’s function for the sphere}$^*$.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[i)] Show that the Dirichlet Green’s function for the Laplacian for the \textbf{interior} of a spherical domain of radius $a$ is
\[
G(\mathbf{x};\mathbf{x}_0)=\frac{-1}{4\pi|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_0|}+\frac{a}{|\mathbf{x}_0|}\frac{1}{4\pi|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_0^*|},\qquad \text{where}\qquad \mathbf{x}_0^*=\frac{a^2\mathbf{x}_0}{|\mathbf{x}_0|^2}.
\]
\item[ii)] Derive the Dirichlet Green’s function for the Laplacian for the \textbf{exterior} of a spherical domain of radius $a$.
\end{enumerate}
```
