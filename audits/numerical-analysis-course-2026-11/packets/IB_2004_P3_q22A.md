## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Given $f\in C^3[0,1]$, we approximate $f'(\tfrac13)$ by the linear combination
\[
\mathcal T[f]=-\frac53 f(0)+\frac43 f(\tfrac12)+\frac13 f(1).
\]
By finding the Peano kernel, determine the least constant $c$ such that
\[
\left|\mathcal T[f]-f'(\tfrac13)\right|\le c\big\|f^{{}'''}\big\|_{\infty}.
\]
```
