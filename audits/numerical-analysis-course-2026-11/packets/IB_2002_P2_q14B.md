## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $f\in C[a,b]$ and let $n+1$ distinct points $x_0,\ldots,x_n\in[a,b]$ be given.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Define the divided difference $f[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ of order $n$ in terms of interpolating polynomials. Prove that it is a symmetric function of the variables $x_i$, $i=0,\ldots,n$.
\item[(b)] Prove the recurrence relation
\[
f[x_0,\ldots,x_n]=\frac{f[x_1,\ldots,x_n]-f[x_0,\ldots,x_{n-1}]}{x_n-x_0}.
\]
\item[(c)] Hence or otherwise deduce that, for any $i\ne j$, we have
\[
f[x_0,\ldots,x_n]=\frac{f[x_0,\ldots,x_{i-1},x_{i+1},\ldots,x_n]-f[x_0,\ldots,x_{j-1},x_{j+1},\ldots,x_n]}{x_j-x_i}.
\]
\item[(d)] From the formulas above, show that, for any $i=1,\ldots,n-1$,
\[
f[x_0,\ldots,x_{i-1},x_{i+1},\ldots,x_n]=\gamma f[x_0,\ldots,x_{n-1}]+(1-\gamma)f[x_1,\ldots,x_n],
\]
where $\gamma=\dfrac{x_i-x_0}{x_n-x_0}$.
\end{enumerate}
```
