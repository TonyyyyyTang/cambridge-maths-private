## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_n\in[a,b]\subset\mathbf R$ are pointwise distinct and $f(x)$ is continuous on $[a,b]$. For $k=1,2,\ldots,n$ define
\[
I_{0,k}(x)=\frac{f(x_0)(x_k-x)-f(x_k)(x_0-x)}{x_k-x_0},
\]
and for $k=2,3,\ldots,n$
\[
I_{0,1,\ldots,k-2,k-1,k}(x)=\frac{I_{0,1,\ldots,k-2,k-1}(x)(x_k-x)-I_{0,1,\ldots,k-2,k}(x)(x_{k-1}-x)}{x_k-x_{k-1}}.
\]
Show that $I_{0,1,\ldots,k-2,k-1,k}(x)$ is a polynomial of order $k$ which interpolates $f(x)$ at $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_k$.

Given $x_k=\{-1,0,2,5\}$ and $f(x_k)=\{33,5,9,1335\}$, determine the interpolating polynomial.
```
