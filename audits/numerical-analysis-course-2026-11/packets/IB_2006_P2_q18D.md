## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
(a) For a positive weight function $w$, let
\[
\int_{-1}^1 f(x)w(x)\,dx\approx\sum_{i=0}^na_if(x_i)
\]
be the corresponding Gaussian quadrature with $n+1$ nodes. Prove that all the coefficients $a_i$ are positive.

(b) The integral
\[
I(f)=\int_{-1}^1 f(x)w(x)\,dx
\]
is approximated by a quadrature
\[
I_n(f)=\sum_{i=0}^na_i^{(n)}f(x_i^{(n)})
\]
which is exact on polynomials of degree $\leqslant n$ and has positive coefficients $a_i^{(n)}$. Prove that, for any $f$ continuous on $[-1,1]$, the quadrature converges to the integral, i.e.,
\[
|I(f)-I_n(f)|\to0\quad\text{as}\quad n\to\infty.
\]

[\emph{You may use the Weierstrass theorem: for any $f$ continuous on $[-1,1]$, and for any $\epsilon>0$, there exists a polynomial $Q$ of degree $n=n(\epsilon,f)$ such that $\displaystyle\max_{x\in[-1,1]}|f(x)-Q(x)|<\epsilon$.}]
```
