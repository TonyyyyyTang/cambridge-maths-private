## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
(a) What are real \emph{orthogonal polynomials} defined with respect to an inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$? What does it mean for such polynomials to be \emph{monic}?

(b) Real monic orthogonal polynomials, $p_n(x)$, of degree $n=0,1,2,\ldots$, are defined with respect to the inner product,
\[
\langle p,q\rangle=\int_{-1}^1w(x)p(x)q(x)\,dx,
\]
where $w(x)$ is a positive weight function. Show that such polynomials obey the three-term recurrence relation,
\[
p_{n+1}(x)=(x-\alpha_n)p_n(x)-\beta_n p_{n-1}(x),
\]
for appropriate $\alpha_n$ and $\beta_n$ which should be given in terms of inner products.
```
