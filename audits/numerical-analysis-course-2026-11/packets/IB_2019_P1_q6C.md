## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $[a,b]$ be the smallest interval that contains the $n+1$ distinct real numbers $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_n$, and let $f$ be a continuous function on that interval.

Define the \emph{divided difference} $f[x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_m]$ of degree $m\leq n$.

Prove that the polynomial of degree $n$ that interpolates the function $f$ at the points $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_n$ is equal to the Newton polynomial
\[
p_n(x)=f[x_0]+f[x_0,x_1](x-x_0)+\cdots+f[x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_n]\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(x-x_i).
\]

Prove the recursive formula
\[
f[x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_m]=\frac{f[x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_m]-f[x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_{m-1}]}{x_m-x_0}
\]
for $1\leq m\leq n$.
```
