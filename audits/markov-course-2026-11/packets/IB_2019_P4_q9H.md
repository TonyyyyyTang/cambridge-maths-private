## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
For a Markov chain $X$ on a state space $S$ with $u,v\in S$, we let $p_{uv}(n)$ for $n\in\{0,1,\ldots\}$ be the probability that $X_n=v$ when $X_0=u$.

(a) Let $X$ be a Markov chain. Prove that if $X$ is recurrent at a state $v$, then $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p_{vv}(n)=\infty$. [You may use without proof that the number of returns of a Markov chain to a state $v$ when starting from $v$ has the geometric distribution.]

(b) Let $X$ and $Y$ be independent simple symmetric random walks on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ starting from the origin $0$. Let $Z=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathbf{1}_{\{X_n=Y_n\}}$. Prove that $\mathbb{E}[Z]=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p_{00}(2n)$ and deduce that $\mathbb{E}[Z]=\infty$. [You may use without proof that $p_{xy}(n)=p_{yx}(n)$ for all $x,y\in\mathbb{Z}^2$ and $n\in\mathbb{N}$, and that $X$ is recurrent at $0$.]
```
