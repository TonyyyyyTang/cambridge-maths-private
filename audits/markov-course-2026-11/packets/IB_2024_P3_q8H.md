## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $\{X_n:n\ge1\}$ be independent, identically distributed, integer-valued random variables. Define

(i) $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$

(ii) $L_n=\min\{X_1,X_2,\ldots,X_n\}$

(iii) $K_n=X_n+X_{n-1}$, with $X_0=0$.

Which of the sequences $\{X_n\},\{S_n\},\{L_n\},\{K_n\}$ are necessarily Markov chains? Justify your answers.
```
