## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
An opera singer is due to perform a long series of concerts. Having a fine artistic temperament, she is liable to pull out each night with probability $1/2$. Once this has happened she will not sing again until the promoter convinces her of his high regard. This he does by sending flowers every day until she returns. Flowers costing $x$ thousand pounds, $0\le x\le1$, bring about a reconciliation with probability $\sqrt{x}$. The promoter stands to make £750 from each successful concert. How much should he spend on flowers?
```
