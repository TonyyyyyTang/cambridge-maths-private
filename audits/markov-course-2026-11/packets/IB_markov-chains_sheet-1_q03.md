## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $X=(X_n)_{n\ge0}$ be a Markov chain and let $(n_r)_{r\ge0}$ be an unbounded increasing sequence of positive integers. Show that $Y_r=X_{n_r}$ defines a (possibly inhomogeneous) Markov chain. Find the transition probabilities of $Y$ when $n_r=2r$ and $X$ is a simple random walk.
```
