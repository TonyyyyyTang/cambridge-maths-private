## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $(X_n)_{n\geqslant0}$ be the symmetric random walk on vertices of a connected graph. At each step this walk jumps from the current vertex to a neighbouring vertex, choosing uniformly amongst them. Let $T_i=\inf\{n\geqslant1:X_n=i\}$. For each $i\ne j$ let $q_{ij}=P(T_j<T_i\mid X_0=i)$ and $m_{ij}=E(T_j\mid X_0=i)$. Stating any theorems that you use:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Prove that the invariant distribution $\pi$ satisfies detailed balance.
\item[(ii)] Use reversibility to explain why $\pi_i q_{ij}=\pi_j q_{ji}$ for all $i,j$.
\end{enumerate}

\item[(iii)] Find $m_{33}$.
\item[(iv)] The removal of any edge $(i,j)$ leaves two disjoint components, one which includes $i$ and one which includes $j$. Prove that $m_{ij}=1+2e_{ij}(i)$, where $e_{ij}(i)$ is the number of edges in the component that contains $i$.
\item[(v)] Show that $m_{ij}+m_{ji}\in\{18,36,54,72\}$ for all $i\ne j$.
\end{enumerate}
```
