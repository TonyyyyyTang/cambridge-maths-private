## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose $(X_n)_{n\ge0}$ is a Markov chain with state space $I$ and transition matrix $P$. What does it mean for $C\subseteq I$ to be a \emph{communicating class}? What does it mean for $C$ to be \emph{closed}?

Consider the Markov chain with state space $\{1,\ldots,6\}$ and transition matrix
\[
P=\begin{pmatrix}
0&\frac13&\frac13&\frac16&0&\frac16\\
\frac13&\frac23&0&0&0&0\\
0&\frac13&\frac13&0&\frac13&0\\
0&0&0&\frac13&\frac23&0\\
0&0&0&\frac12&\frac12&0\\
\frac12&0&\frac12&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Determine the communicating classes. Which of these are closed?

What is the probability that the chain ever reaches $2$, given that it starts in $1$?
```
