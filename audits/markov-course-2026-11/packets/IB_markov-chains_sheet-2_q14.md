## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
In each of the following cases determine whether the stochastic matrix $P$ corresponds to a chain which is reversible in equilibrium:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\begin{pmatrix}p&1-p\\ q&1-q\end{pmatrix}$;
\item[(b)] $\begin{pmatrix}0&p&1-p\\ 1-p&0&p\\ p&1-p&0\end{pmatrix}$;
\item[(c)] $I=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and $p_{01}=1$, $p_{i,i+1}=p$, $p_{i,i-1}=1-p$ for $i\ge1$.
\item[(d)] $p_{ij}=p_{ji}$ for all $i,j\in I$.
\end{enumerate}
```
