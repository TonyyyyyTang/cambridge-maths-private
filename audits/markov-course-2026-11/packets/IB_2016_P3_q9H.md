## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $(X_n)_{n\geq0}$ be a Markov chain such that $X_0=i$. Prove that
\[
\sum_{n=0}^\infty\mathbb{P}_i(X_n=i)=\frac{1}{\mathbb{P}_i(X_n\ne i\text{ for all }n\geq1)}
\]
where $1/0=+\infty$. [You may use the strong Markov property without proof.]
```
