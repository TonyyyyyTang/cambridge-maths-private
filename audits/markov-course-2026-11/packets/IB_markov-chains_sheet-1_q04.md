## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
(Harder) Let $S=(S_n)_{n\ge0}$ be a simple (possibly asymmetric) random walk on $\Z$ with $S_0=0$. Show that $X_n=|S_n|$ defines a Markov chain and find its transition probabilities. Let $M_n=\max_{k\le n}S_k$ and show that $Y_n=M_n-S_n$ defines a Markov chain.
```
