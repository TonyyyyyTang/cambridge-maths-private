## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A random walk on the set $\{0,1,2,\ldots,b\}$ has transition matrix given by $p_{00}=1-\lambda_0$, $p_{bb}=1-\mu_b$, $p_{i,i+1}=\lambda_i$ and $p_{i+1,i}=\mu_{i+1}$ for $0\le i<b$, where $0<\lambda_i,\mu_i<1$ for all $i$, and $\lambda_i+\mu_i=1$ for $1\le i<b$. Show that this process is time-reversible in equilibrium.
```
