## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose that $B$ is a non-empty subset of the statespace $I$ of a Markov chain $X$ with transition matrix $P$, and let $\tau\equiv\inf\{n\geqslant0:X_n\in B\}$, with the convention that $\inf\emptyset=\infty$. If $h_i=P(\tau<\infty|X_0=i)$, show that the equations
\[\begin{aligned}(a)\qquad g_i&\geqslant(Pg)_i\equiv\sum_{j\in I}p_{ij}g_j\geqslant0\quad\forall i,\\(b)\qquad g_i&=1\quad\forall i\in B\end{aligned}\]
are satisfied by $g=h$.

If $g$ satisfies (a), prove that $g$ also satisfies
\[(c)\qquad g_i\geqslant(\tilde P g)_i\quad\forall i,\]
where
\[\tilde p_{ij}=\begin{cases}p_{ij}&(i\notin B),\\\delta_{ij}&(i\in B).\end{cases}\]
By interpreting the transition matrix $\tilde P$, prove that $h$ is the minimal solution to the equations (a), (b).

Now suppose that $P$ is irreducible. Prove that $P$ is recurrent if and only if the only solutions to (a) are constant functions.
```
