## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $B_1,B_2,\ldots$ be disjoint events with $\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}B_n=\Omega$. Show that if $A$ is another event and $P(A\mid B_n)=p$ for all $n$ then $P(A)=p$. Deduce that if $X$ and $Y$ are discrete random variables then the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $X$ and $Y$ are independent,
\item[(b)] the conditional distribution of $X$ given $Y=y$ is independent of $y$.
\end{enumerate}
```
