## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A Markov chain has state space $\{a,b,c\}$ and transition matrix
\[
P=\begin{pmatrix}0&3/5&2/5\\3/4&0&1/4\\2/3&1/3&0\end{pmatrix},
\]
where the rows 1,2,3 correspond to $a,b,c$, respectively. Show that this Markov chain is equivalent to a random walk on some graph with 6 edges.

Let $k(i,j)$ denote the mean first passage time from $i$ to $j$.

(i) Find $k(a,a)$ and $k(a,b)$.

(ii) Given $X_0=a$, find the expected number of steps until the walk first completes a step from $b$ to $c$.

(iii) Suppose the distribution of $X_0$ is $(\pi_1,\pi_2,\pi_3)=(5,4,3)/12$. Let $\tau(a,b)$ be the least $m$ such that $\{a,b\}$ appears as a subsequence of $\{X_0,X_1,\ldots,X_m\}$. By comparing the distributions of $\{X_0,X_1,\ldots,X_m\}$ and $\{X_m,\ldots,X_1,X_0\}$ show that $E\tau(a,b)=E\tau(b,a)$ and that
\[
k(b,a)-k(a,b)=\sum_{i\in\{a,b,c\}}\pi_i\big[k(i,a)-k(i,b)\big].
\]
```
