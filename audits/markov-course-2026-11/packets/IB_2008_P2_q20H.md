## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A Markov chain with state–space $I=\mathbb Z^+$ has non-zero transition probabilities $p_{00}=q_0$ and
\[p_{i,i+1}=p_i,\quad p_{i+1,i}=q_{i+1}\qquad(i\in I).\]
Prove that this chain is recurrent if and only if
\[\sum_{n\geqslant1}\prod_{r=1}^n\frac{q_r}{p_r}=\infty.\]
Prove that this chain is positive-recurrent if and only if
\[\sum_{n\geqslant1}\prod_{r=1}^n\frac{p_{r-1}}{q_r}<\infty.\]
```
