## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Given that $f(z)$ is an analytic function, show that the mapping $w=f(z)$

(a) preserves angles between smooth curves intersecting at $z$ if $f'(z)\neq0$;

(b) has Jacobian given by $|f'(z)|^2$.
```
