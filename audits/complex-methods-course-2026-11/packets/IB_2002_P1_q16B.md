## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that if $f$ is an analytic function at $z_0$ and $f'(z_0)\ne0$, then $f$ is conformal at $z_0$, i.e. it preserves angles between paths passing through $z_0$.
\item[(b)] Let $D$ be the disc given by $|z+i|<\sqrt2$, and let $H$ be the half-plane given by $y>0$, where $z=x+iy$. Construct a map of the domain $D\cap H$ onto $H$, and hence find a conformal mapping of $D\cap H$ onto the disc $\{z:|z|<1\}$. [\emph{Hint: You may find it helpful to consider a mapping of the form $(az+b)/(cz+d)$, where $ad-bc\ne0$.}]
\end{enumerate}
```
