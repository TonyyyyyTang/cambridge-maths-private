## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $H$ be the domain $\mathbb C-\{x+iy:x\le0,y=0\}$ (i.e., $\mathbb C$ cut along the negative $x$-axis). Show, by a suitable choice of branch, that the mapping
\[
z\mapsto w=-i\log z
\]
maps $H$ onto the strip $S=\{z=x+iy,-\pi<x<\pi\}$.

How would a different choice of branch change the result?

Let $G$ be the domain $\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1,|z+i|>\sqrt2\}$. Find an analytic transformation that maps $G$ to $S$, where $S$ is the strip defined above.
```
