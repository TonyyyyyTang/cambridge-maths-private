## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the Laplace transform of
\[
y(t)=\frac{e^{-a^2/4t}}{\sqrt{\pi t}},
\]
for $a\in\mathbb{R}$, $a\ne 0$.

[\emph{You may use without proof that}
\[
\int_0^\infty\exp\left(-c^2x^2-\frac{c^2}{x^2}\right)dx=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2|c|}e^{-2c^2}.\,]
\]
\item[(b)] By using the Laplace transform, show that the solution to
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}&=\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\qquad -\infty<x<\infty,\quad t>0,\\
u(x,0)&=f(x),\\
u(x,t)&\quad\text{bounded},
\end{aligned}
\]
can be written as
\[
u(x,t)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}K(|x-\xi|,t)f(\xi)d\xi
\]
for some $K(|x-\xi|,t)$ to be determined.

[\emph{You may use without proof that a particular solution to}
\[
y''(x)-sy(x)+f(x)=0
\]
\emph{is given by}
\[
y(x)=\frac{e^{-\sqrt{s}\,x}}{2\sqrt{s}}\int_0^x e^{\sqrt{s}\,\xi}f(\xi)\,d\xi-\frac{e^{\sqrt{s}\,x}}{2\sqrt{s}}\int_0^x e^{-\sqrt{s}\,\xi}f(\xi)\,d\xi.\,]
\]
\end{enumerate}
```
