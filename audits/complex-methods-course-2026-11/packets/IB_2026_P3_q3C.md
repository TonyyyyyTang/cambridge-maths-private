## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Consider the real integral
\[
I=\int_0^\infty\frac{dx}{1+x^p},
\]
where $p>1$ is a real number. Use a wedge-shaped contour (including the lines $\arg z=0$ and $\arg z=2\pi/p$) and the residue theorem to show that
\[
I=\frac{\pi}{p}\operatorname{cosec}\left(\frac{\pi}{p}\right).
\]
```
