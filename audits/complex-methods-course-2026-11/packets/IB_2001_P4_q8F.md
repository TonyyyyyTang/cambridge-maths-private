## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Consider a conformal mapping of the form
\[
f(z)=\frac{a+bz}{c+dz},\quad z\in\C,
\]
where $a,b,c,d\in\C,$ and $ad\ne bc$. You may assume $b\ne0$. Show that any such $f(z)$ which maps the unit circle onto itself is necessarily of the form
\[
f(z)=e^{i\psi}\frac{a+z}{1+\bar az}\cdot
\]
[\emph{Hint: Show that it is always possible to choose $b=1$.}]
```
