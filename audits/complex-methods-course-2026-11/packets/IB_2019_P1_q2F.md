## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
What is the \emph{Laurent series} for a function $f$ defined in an annulus $A$? Find the Laurent series for $f(z)=\frac{10}{(z+2)(z^2+1)}$ on the annuli
\[
\begin{aligned}A_1&=\{z\in\mathbb{C}\mid 0<|z|<1\}\quad\text{and}\\
A_2&=\{z\in\mathbb{C}\mid 1<|z|<2\}.\end{aligned}
\]
```
