## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Prove by using the Cauchy theorem that if $f$ is analytic in the open disc $\Omega=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ then there exists a function $g$, analytic in $\Omega$, such that $g'(z)=f(z)$, $z\in\Omega$.
```
