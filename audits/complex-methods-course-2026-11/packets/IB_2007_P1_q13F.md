## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
By integrating round the contour $C_R$, which is the boundary of the domain
\[
D_R=\{z=re^{i\theta}:0<r<R,\quad 0<\theta<\dfrac\pi4\},
\]
evaluate each of the integrals
\[
\int_0^\infty\sin x^2\,dx,\quad\int_0^\infty\cos x^2\,dx.
\]
[\emph{You may use the relations $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty e^{-r^2}\,dr=\frac{\sqrt\pi}2$ and $\sin t\ge\dfrac2\pi t$ for $0\le t\le\frac\pi2.\bigr]$}
```
