## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
For the function
\[
f(z)=\frac{2z}{z^2+1},
\]
determine the Taylor series of $f$ around the point $z_0=1$, and give the largest $r$ for which this series converges in the disc $|z-1|<r$.
```
