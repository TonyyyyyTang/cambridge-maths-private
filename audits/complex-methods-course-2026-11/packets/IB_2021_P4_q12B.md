## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $f(t)$ be defined for $t\ge0$. Define the \emph{Laplace transform} $\widehat f(s)$ of $f$. Find an expression for the Laplace transform of $\frac{df}{dt}$ in terms of $\widehat f$.

Three radioactive nuclei decay sequentially, so that the numbers $N_i(t)$ of the three types obey the equations
\[
\begin{aligned}\frac{dN_1}{dt}&=-\lambda_1N_1,\\\frac{dN_2}{dt}&=\lambda_1N_1-\lambda_2N_2,\\\frac{dN_3}{dt}&=\lambda_2N_2-\lambda_3N_3,\end{aligned}
\]
where $\lambda_3>\lambda_2>\lambda_1>0$ are constants. Initially, at $t=0$, $N_1=N$, $N_2=0$ and $N_3=n$. Using Laplace transforms, find $N_3(t)$.

By taking an appropriate limit, find $N_3(t)$ when $\lambda_2=\lambda_1=\lambda>0$ and $\lambda_3>\lambda$.
```
