## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{Laplace’s equation.} Show that the inverse Fourier transform of the function (for any real $\alpha$)
\[
\widetilde f_\alpha(k)=\begin{cases}e^{k\alpha}-e^{-k\alpha},&|k|\le1,\\0&|k|>1,\end{cases}
\]
is
\[
f_\alpha(x)=\frac{2i}{\pi(\alpha^2+x^2)}(\alpha\cosh\alpha\sin x-x\cos x\sinh\alpha).
\]
Determine, by using Fourier transforms, the solution of Laplace’s equation in the infinite strip $0\le y\le 1$, i.e.
\[
\nabla^2\psi=0;\qquad-\infty<x<\infty,\quad 0<y<1,
\]
where $\psi(x,0)=f_1(x)$ the function given above with $\alpha=1$, and $\psi(x,1)=0$ for $-\infty<x<\infty$.
```
