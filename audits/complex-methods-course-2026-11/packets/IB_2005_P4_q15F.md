## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Determine the Fourier expansion of the function $f(x)=\sin\lambda x$, where $-\pi\leqslant x\leqslant\pi$, in the two cases where $\lambda$ is an integer and $\lambda$ is a real non-integer.

Using the Parseval identity in the case $\lambda=\frac12$, find an explicit expression for the sum $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{n^2}{(4n^2-1)^2}.$
```
