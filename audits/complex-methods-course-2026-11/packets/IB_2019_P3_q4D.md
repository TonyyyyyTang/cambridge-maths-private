## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
By considering the transformation $w=i(1-z)/(1+z)$, find a solution to Laplace's equation $\nabla^2\phi=0$ inside the unit disc $D\subset\mathbb{C}$, subject to the boundary conditions
\[
\left.\phi\right|_{|z|=1}=\begin{cases}\phi_0&\text{for }\arg(z)\in(0,\pi)\\-\phi_0&\text{for }\arg(z)\in(\pi,2\pi),\end{cases}
\]
where $\phi_0$ is constant. Give your answer in terms of $(x,y)=(\operatorname{Re}z,\operatorname{Im}z)$.
```
