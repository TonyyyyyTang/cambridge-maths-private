## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
By using the Laplace transform, show that the solution to
\[
y''-4y'+3y=t\,e^{-3t},
\]
subject to the conditions $y(0)=0$ and $y'(0)=1$, is given by
\[
y(t)=\frac{37}{72}e^{3t}-\frac{17}{32}e^t+\left(\frac{5}{288}+\frac1{24}t\right)e^{-3t}
\]
when $t\geq0$.
```
