## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
An incompressible viscous fluid of constant uniform viscosity $\mu$ and density $\rho$ undergoes unidirectional flow of the form $\mathbf u=u(y,t)\mathbf e_x$ in two dimensions. Gravity is negligible.

(a) Use a small control fluid volume of size $\delta x\times\delta y$,
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] to show that this flow satisfies mass conservation;
\item[(ii)] to derive the momentum conservation equation satisfied by $u(y,t)$ and the pressure $p(x)$.
\end{enumerate}

(b) The flow is steady, is subject to a uniform pressure gradient $G=\mathrm dp/\mathrm dx$ and occurs between two rigid surfaces at $y=0$ and $y=h$. The surface at $y=0$ is stationary while the surface at $y=h$ translates with velocity $U\mathbf e_x$, where $U$ is a constant parameter.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Solve for the flow $u(y)$ in terms of $G$ and $U$.
\item[(ii)] Compute the value $G_0$ of the applied pressure gradient $G$ for which the shear stress at $y=0$ is zero.
\item[(iii)] For $G=G_0$, deduce the volume flux in the $x$ direction.
\item[(iv)] For $G=G_0$, use $u(y)$ to compute the shear stress exerted by the flow on the top plate. Show that it can also be obtained by using a force balance on a small control fluid volume of size $\delta x\times h$.
\end{enumerate}
```
