## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A two-dimensional cylinder of radius $a$ is stationary in a uniform flow of velocity $U\mathbf e_x$. The flow is assumed to be steady, inviscid, two-dimensional and irrotational. There is no circulation around the cylinder.

Using a velocity potential, solve for the flow $\mathbf u(r,\theta)$ around the cylinder. Use Bernoulli’s equation to compute the pressure on its surface as a function of the polar angle $\theta$.
```
