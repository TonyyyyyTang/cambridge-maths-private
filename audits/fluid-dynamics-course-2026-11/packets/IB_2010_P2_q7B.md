## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Write down an expression for the velocity field of a line vortex of strength $\kappa$.

Consider $N$ identical line vortices of strength $\kappa$ arranged at equal intervals round a circle of radius $a$. Show that the vortices all move around the circle at constant angular velocity $(N-1)\kappa/(4\pi a^2)$.
```
