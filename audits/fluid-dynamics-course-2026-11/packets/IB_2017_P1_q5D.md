## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
For each of the flows

(i) $\mathbf{u}=(2xy,x^2+y^2)$

(ii) $\mathbf{u}=(-2y,-2x)$

determine whether or not the flow is incompressible and/or irrotational. Find the associated velocity potential and/or stream function when appropriate. For either \textbf{one} of the flows, sketch the streamlines of the flow, indicating the direction of the flow.
```
