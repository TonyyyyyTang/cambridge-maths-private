## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Consider the $2\times2$ hermitian matrices defined by
\[
S_1=\frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
S_2=\frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
S_3=\frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Evaluate the commutators $[S_i,S_j]$ for all values of $i$ and $j$ (there are only three independent cases to consider) and calculate the matrix $S^2=S_1^2+S_2^2+S_3^2$.

Write down simultaneous eigenvectors of $S_3$ and $S^2$ and hence show that their eigenvalues are $\pm\hbar s$ and $\hbar^2s(s+1)$, respectively, for a certain positive number $s$.
```
