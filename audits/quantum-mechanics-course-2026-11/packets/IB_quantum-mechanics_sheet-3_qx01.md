## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
As a model for the deuterium nucleus (a bound state of a proton and a neutron) consider a particle in the 3-dimensional square-well potential
\[
U(r)=\begin{cases}-U_0&r<a\\0&r>a\end{cases}
\]
with $U_0>0$. Show how to find the spherically symmetric bound-state wavefunctions. Is there always a bound state? [\emph{You may use $\nabla^2\psi=r^{-1}d^2(r\psi)/dr^2$.}]
```
