## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Write down expressions for the probability density $\rho(x,t)$ and the probability current $j(x,t)$ for a particle in one dimension with wavefunction $\Psi(x,t)$. If $\Psi(x,t)$ obeys the time-dependent Schrödinger equation with a real potential, show that
\[
\frac{\partial j}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}=0.
\]
Consider a stationary state, $\Psi(x,t)=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$, with
\[
\psi(x)\sim\begin{cases}e^{ik_1x}+Re^{-ik_1x}&x\to-\infty\\Te^{ik_2x}&x\to+\infty\end{cases},
\]
where $E$, $k_1$, $k_2$ are real. Evaluate $j(x,t)$ for this state in the regimes $x\to+\infty$ and $x\to-\infty$.

Consider a real potential,
\[
V(x)=-\alpha\delta(x)+U(x),\qquad U(x)=\begin{cases}0&x<0\\V_0&x>0\end{cases},
\]
where $\delta(x)$ is the Dirac delta function, $V_0>0$ and $\alpha>0$. Assuming that $\psi(x)$ is continuous at $x=0$, derive an expression for
\[
\lim_{\epsilon\to0}\left[\psi'(\epsilon)-\psi'(-\epsilon)\right].
\]
Hence calculate the reflection and transmission probabilities for a particle incident from $x=-\infty$ with energy $E>V_0$.
```
