## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A particle of mass $m$ moves in a one-dimensional potential defined by
\[
V(x)=\begin{cases}\infty&\text{for }x<0,\\0&\text{for }0\leqslant x\leqslant a,\\V_0&\text{for }a<x,\end{cases}
\]
where $a$ and $V_0$ are positive constants. Defining $c=[2m(V_0-E)]^{1/2}/\hbar$ and $k=(2mE)^{1/2}/\hbar$, show that for any allowed positive value $E$ of the energy with $E<V_0$ then
\[
c+k\cot ka=0.
\]
Find the minimum value of $V_0$ for this equation to have a solution.

Find the normalized wave function for the particle. Write down an expression for the expectation value of $x$ in terms of two integrals, which you need not evaluate. Given that
\[
\langle x\rangle=\frac1{2k}(ka-\tan ka),
\]
discuss briefly the possibility of $\langle x\rangle$ being greater than $a$. [Hint: consider the graph of $-ka\cot ka$ against $ka$.]
```
