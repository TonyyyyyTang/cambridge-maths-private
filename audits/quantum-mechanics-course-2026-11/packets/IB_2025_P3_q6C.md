## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $\psi(x,t)$ solve the time-dependent Schrödinger equation
\[
i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial t}=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2\psi}{\partial x^2}+\frac12kx^2\psi,\qquad -\infty<x<+\infty,
\]
for a particle of mass $m$ moving in a potential $V(x)=\frac12kx^2$. If $\mathcal O$ is an operator representing an observable, its expectation value at time $t$ in a normalized state $\psi$ is
\[
\langle\mathcal O\rangle(t)=\int\psi(x,t)^*\mathcal O\psi(x,t)dx.
\]
Write down operators $Q$ and $P$ representing, respectively, the position and the momentum of the particle. Calculate the time derivative of $\langle P\rangle(t)$ as a function of $\langle Q\rangle(t)$ and interpret the answer.

\emph{[Hint: You may assume $\psi$ and its derivatives are smooth and decrease to zero at infinity as needed.]}
```
