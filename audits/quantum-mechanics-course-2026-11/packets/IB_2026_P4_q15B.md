## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Consider a wavefunction $\psi(x,t)$ describing a particle of mass $m$ moving in one dimension.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Write down expressions for the probability density $\rho$ and current density $j$.
\item[(ii)] Starting from the time-dependent Schrödinger equation, show that $\rho$ and $j$ satisfy
\[
\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}+\frac{\partial j}{\partial x}=0.
\]
For
\[
\psi(x,t)=\Psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar},
\]
show that the current is a constant in $x$.
\item[(iii)] A particle of mass $m$ moving in one dimension is incident from the left with energy $E=3U_0/2$ on a potential well $U(x)$ given by
\[
U(x)=\begin{cases}
0,&0\le x\le a,\\
U_0,&x<0,\ x>a,
\end{cases}
\]
where $U_0$ is a positive constant. Write down the conditions that should be imposed on the wavefunction at $x=0$ and $x=a$. Use these conditions to determine that the probability of transmission to the right is
\[
\frac1{1+F\sin^2(G\sqrt{U_0})},
\]
where $F$ and $G$ are constants which you should determine.
\item[(iv)] Keeping $E=3U_0/2$, for what values of $U_0$ is the probability of transmission minimised?
\end{enumerate}
```
