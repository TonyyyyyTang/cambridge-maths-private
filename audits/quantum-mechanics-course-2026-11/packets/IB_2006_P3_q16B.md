## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
The expression $\Delta_\psi A$ denotes the uncertainty of a quantum mechanical observable $A$ in a state with normalised wavefunction $\psi$. Prove that the Heisenberg uncertainty principle
\[
(\Delta_\psi x)(\Delta_\psi p)\geqslant\frac{\hbar}{2}
\]
holds for all normalised wavefunctions $\psi(x)$ of one spatial dimension.

[\emph{You may quote Schwarz’s inequality without proof.}]

A Gaussian wavepacket evolves so that at time $t$ its wavefunction is
\[
\psi(x,t)=(2\pi)^{-\frac14}\Bigl(1+i\hbar t\Bigr)^{-\frac12}\exp\Bigl(-\frac{x^2}{4(1+i\hbar t)}\Bigr).
\]
Calculate the uncertainties $\Delta_\psi x$ and $\Delta_\psi p$ at each time $t$, and hence verify explicitly that the uncertainty principle holds at each time $t$.

$\Bigl[$\emph{You may quote without proof the results that if $\operatorname{Re}(a)>0$ then}
\[
\int_{-\infty}^\infty\exp\left(-\frac{x^2}{a^*}\right)x^2\exp\left(-\frac{x^2}{a}\right)\,dx=\frac14\left(\frac\pi2\right)^{\frac12}\frac{|a|^3}{(\operatorname{Re}(a))^{\frac32}}
\]
\emph{and}
\[
\int_{-\infty}^\infty\left(\frac d{dx}\exp\left(-\frac{x^2}{a^*}\right)\right)\left(\frac d{dx}\exp\left(-\frac{x^2}{a}\right)\right)\,dx=\left(\frac\pi2\right)^{\frac12}\frac{|a|}{(\operatorname{Re}(a))^{\frac32}}.\,\Bigr]
\]
```
