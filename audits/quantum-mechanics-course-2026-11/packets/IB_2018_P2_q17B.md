## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
For an electron in a hydrogen atom, the stationary-state wavefunctions are of the form $\psi(r,\theta,\phi)=R(r)Y_{lm}(\theta,\phi)$, where in suitable units $R$ obeys the radial equation
\[
\frac{d^2R}{dr^2}+\frac{2}{r}\frac{dR}{dr}-\frac{l(l+1)}{r^2}R+2\left(E+\frac{1}{r}\right)R=0.
\]
Explain briefly how the terms in this equation arise.

This radial equation has bound-state solutions of energy $E=E_n$, where $E_n=-\frac{1}{2n^2}$ ($n=1,2,3,\ldots$). Show that when $l=n-1$, there is a solution of the form $R(r)=r^\alpha e^{-r/n}$, and determine $\alpha$. Find the expectation value $\langle r\rangle$ in this state.

Determine the total degeneracy of the energy level with energy $E_n$.
```
