## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Write down a formula for the orbital angular momentum operator $\hat{\mathbf L}$. Show that its components satisfy
\[
[L_i,L_j]=i\hbar\,\epsilon_{ijk}\,L_k.
\]
If $L_3\psi=0$, show that $(L_1\pm iL_2)\psi$ are also eigenvectors of $L_3$, and find their eigenvalues.
```
