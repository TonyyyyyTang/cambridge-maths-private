## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A one-dimensional system has the potential
\[
V(x)=\Biggl\{\begin{aligned}&0&&\quad x<0\text{,}\\[-1pt]&\tfrac{\hbar^2 U}{2m}&&\quad 0<x<L\text{,}\\[-1pt]&0&&\quad x>L\text{.}\end{aligned}
\]
For energy $E=\hbar^2\epsilon/(2m)$, $\epsilon<U$, the wave function has the form
\[
\psi(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&a\,e^{ikx}+c\,e^{-ikx}&&\quad x<0\text{,}\\[-3pt]&e\cosh Kx+f\sinh Kx&&\quad 0<x<L\text{,}\\[-3pt]&d\,e^{ik(x-L)}+b\,e^{-ik(x-L)}&&\quad x>L\text{.}\end{aligned}\right.
\]
By considering the relation between incoming and outgoing waves explain why we should expect
\[
|c|^2+|d|^2=|a|^2+|b|^2.
\]
Find four linear relations between $a,b,c,d,e,f$. Eliminate $d,e,f$ and show that
\[
c=\frac1D\left[b+\frac12\Bigl(\lambda-\frac1\lambda\Bigr)\sinh KL\;a\right],
\]
where $D=\cosh KL-\tfrac12\left(\lambda+\tfrac1\lambda\right)\sinh KL$ and $\lambda=K/(ik)$. By using the result for $c$, or otherwise, explain why the solution for $d$ is
\[
d=\frac1D\left[a+\frac12\Bigl(\lambda-\frac1\lambda\Bigr)\sinh KL\;b\right].
\]
For $b=0$ define the transmission coefficient $T$ and show that, for large $L$,
\[
T\approx16\frac{\epsilon(U-\epsilon)}{U^2}e^{-2\sqrt{U-\epsilon}\,L}.
\]
```
