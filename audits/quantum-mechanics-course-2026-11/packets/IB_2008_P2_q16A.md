## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Give the physical interpretation of the expression
\[\langle A\rangle_\psi=\int\psi(x)^*\hat A\psi(x)dx\]
for an observable $A$, where $\hat A$ is a Hermitian operator and $\psi$ is normalised. By considering the norm of the state $(A+i\lambda B)\psi$ for two observables $A$ and $B$, and real values of $\lambda$, show that
\[\langle A^2\rangle_\psi\langle B^2\rangle_\psi\geqslant\frac14|\langle[A,B]\rangle_\psi|^2.\]
Deduce the uncertainty relation
\[\Delta A\Delta B\geqslant\frac12|\langle[A,B]\rangle_\psi|,\]
where $\Delta A$ is the uncertainty of $A$.

A particle of mass $m$ moves in one dimension under the influence of potential $\tfrac12m\omega^2x^2$. By considering the commutator $[x,p]$, show that the expectation value of the Hamiltonian satisfies
\[\langle H\rangle_\psi\geqslant\frac12\hbar\omega.\]
```
