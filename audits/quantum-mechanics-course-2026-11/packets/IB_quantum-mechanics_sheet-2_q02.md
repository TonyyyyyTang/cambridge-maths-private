## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Write down the time-independent Schrödinger equation for the wavefunction of a particle moving in a potential $U(x)=-U\delta(x)$, where $U$ is a positive constant and $\delta(x)$ is the Dirac delta function. Integrate the equation over the interval $-\epsilon<x<\epsilon$, for a positive constant $\epsilon$, and hence deduce that there is a discontinuity at $x=0$ in the derivative of $\chi(x)$:
\[
\lim_{\epsilon\to0}\big[\chi'(\epsilon)-\chi'(-\epsilon)\big]=-\frac{2mU}{\hbar^2}\chi(0).
\]
By using this condition to relate appropriate solutions for $x>0$ and $x<0$, find the unique bound and normalisable eigenstate of the Hamiltonian, and determine its energy eigenvalue $E$ (with $E<0$).
```
