## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
For the harmonic oscillator in Example 8, write down the time-dependent Schrödinger equation satisfied by $\psi(x,t)$ and give the solution for each of the following initial conditions:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{(i)}\quad&\psi(x,0)=\chi_0(x),\qquad \text{(ii)}\quad\psi(x,0)=\chi_1(x),\qquad
\text{(iii)}\quad\psi(x,0)=\frac12\left(\sqrt3\chi_0(x)-i\chi_1(x)\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
For the solution in case (iii), what is the first time $T>0$ at which $\psi(x,T)$ and $\psi(x,0)$ correspond to physically equivalent states?

[\emph{Hint: Remember that $\psi(x,t)=\sum_n c_n\chi_n(x)e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}$ and that therefore $\psi(x,0)=\sum_n c_n\chi_n(x)$.}]
```
