## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
The quantum mechanical angular momentum operators are
\[
L_i=-i\hbar\,\epsilon_{ijk}\,x_j\,\frac{\partial}{\partial x_k}\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Show that each of these is hermitian.

The total angular momentum operator is defined as $\mathbf{L}^2=L_1^2+L_2^2+L_3^2$. Show that $\langle\mathbf{L}^2\rangle\geqslant\langle L_3^2\rangle$ in any state, and show that the only states where $\langle\mathbf{L}^2\rangle=\langle L_3^2\rangle$ are those with no angular dependence. Verify that the eigenvalues of the operators $\mathbf{L}^2$ and $L_3^2$ (whose values you may quote without proof) are consistent with these results.
```
