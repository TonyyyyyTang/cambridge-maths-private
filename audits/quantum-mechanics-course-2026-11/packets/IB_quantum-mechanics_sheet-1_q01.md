## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
When a sample of potassium is illuminated with light of wavelength $3\times10^{-7}\mathrm{m}$, electrons are emitted with kinetic energy $2.1\ \mathrm{eV}$. When the same sample is illuminated with light of wavelength $5\times10^{-7}\mathrm{m}$ it emits electrons with kinetic energy $0.5\ \mathrm{eV}$. Use Einstein's explanation of this \emph{photoelectric effect} to obtain a value for Planck's constant, and find the minimum energy $W$ needed to free an electron from the surface of potassium.
```
