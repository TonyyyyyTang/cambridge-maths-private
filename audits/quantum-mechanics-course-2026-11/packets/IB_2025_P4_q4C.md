## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A quantum particle of mass $m$ is confined to move inside the rectangular box
\[
\{(x,y,z):0\le x\le a,\ 0\le y\le b,\ 0\le z\le c\}.
\]
Derive the energy eigenvalues and eigenfunctions under the assumption that $a<b<c$. (You need not normalize the eigenfunctions.)

What is the degeneracy of the ground state, i.e., the dimension of the eigenspace corresponding to the lowest energy eigenvalue, and similarly for the next to lowest energy eigenvalue (the first excited state)?

How do your conclusions change if $a<b=c$?
```
