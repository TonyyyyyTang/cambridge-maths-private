## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A quantum system has a complete set of orthonormalised energy eigenfunctions $\psi_n(x)$ with corresponding energy eigenvalues $E_n,$ $n=1,2,3,\ldots$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] If the time-dependent wavefunction $\psi(x,t)$ is, at $t=0$,
\[
\psi(x,0)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n\psi_n(x),
\]
determine $\psi(x,t)$ for all $t>0$.
\item[(b)] A linear operator $\mathcal S$ acts on the energy eigenfunctions as follows:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal S\psi_1&=7\psi_1+24\psi_2,\\
\mathcal S\psi_2&=24\psi_1-7\psi_2,\\
\mathcal S\psi_n&=0,\quad n\geqslant3.
\end{aligned}
\]
Find the eigenvalues of $\mathcal S$. At time $t=0,$ $\mathcal S$ is measured and its lowest eigenvalue is found. At time $t>0,$ $\mathcal S$ is measured again. Show that the probability for obtaining the lowest eigenvalue again is
\[
\frac1{625}\biggl(337+288\cos(\omega t)\biggr),
\]
where $\omega=(E_1-E_2)/\hbar$.
\end{enumerate}
```
