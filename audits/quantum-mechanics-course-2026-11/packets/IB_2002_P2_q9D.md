## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
From the expressions
\[
L_x=yP_z-zP_y,\quad L_y=zP_x-xP_z,\quad L_z=xP_y-yP_x,
\]
show that
\[
(x+iy)z
\]
is an eigenfunction of $\mathbf L^2$ and $L_z$, and compute the corresponding eigenvalues.
```
