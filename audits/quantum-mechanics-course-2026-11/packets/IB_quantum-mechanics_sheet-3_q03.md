## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $\hat A$ and $\hat B$ be hermitian operators. Show that $i[\hat A,\hat B]$ is hermitian.

Given a normalised state $\psi$, consider $\|(\hat A+i\lambda\hat B)\psi\|^2$ with $\lambda$ a real variable and deduce that
\[
\langle\hat A^2\rangle\langle\hat B^2\rangle\geq\tfrac14\left|\langle i[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle\right|^2\,,
\]
with all expectation values taken in the state $\psi$. Hence derive the generalised uncertainty relation:
\[
\Delta A\,\Delta B\geq\tfrac12\left|\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle\right|\,.
\]
```
