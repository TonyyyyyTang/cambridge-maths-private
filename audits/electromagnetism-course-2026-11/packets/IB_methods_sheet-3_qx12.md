## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\emph{The principle of least action$^*$.} Hamilton’s Principle is applicable also to the \emph{relativistic} dynamics of a charged particle in an electromagnetic field. The appropriate choice of Lagrangian $L[t,\mathbf r(t),\dot{\mathbf r}(t)]$ is
\[
L=-m_0 c^2\gamma^{-1}-q\phi+q\mathbf v.\mathbf A,
\]
with the Lorentz factor $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-\frac12}$, and where $\mathbf r$ is the position and $\mathbf v=\dot{\mathbf r}(t)$ is the velocity of a particle of rest-mass $m_0$ and charge $q$ in fields determined by a given scalar potential $\phi(\mathbf r,t)$ and a given vector potential $\mathbf A(\mathbf r,t)$. Verify that the Euler-Lagrange equations, with this choice of $L$, yield the equation of motion
\[
\frac{d}{dt}(m_0\gamma\mathbf v)=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B),
\]
where the electric field $\mathbf E=-\nabla\phi-\frac{\partial\mathbf A}{\partial t}$ and the magnetic field $\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A$.
```
