## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
State Faraday’s law of induction, defining any terms that appear in the equation.

A circular wire loop has resistance $R$ and lies in the $z=0$ plane in a constant magnetic field $\mathbf B=B\hat{\mathbf z}$ with $B>0$. The radius of the loop varies in time as $r(t)$. What is the current in the wire?

Distinguishing the two situations $\dot r(t)>0$ and $\dot r(t)<0$, draw a picture showing the magnetic field due to the induced current. Is the magnetic field increased or decreased inside the loop? In what direction is the Lorentz force on the wire in each case?
```
