## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Write down Maxwell's Equations for electric and magnetic fields $\mathbf{E}(\mathbf{x},t)$ and $\mathbf{B}(\mathbf{x},t)$ in the absence of charges and currents. Show that there are solutions of the form
\[
\mathbf{E}(\mathbf{x},t)=\operatorname{Re}\{\mathbf{E}_0\, e^{i(\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x}-\omega t)}\},\quad
\mathbf{B}(\mathbf{x},t)=\operatorname{Re}\{\mathbf{B}_0\, e^{i(\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{x}-\omega t)}\}
\]
if $\mathbf{E}_0$ and $\mathbf{k}$ satisfy a constraint and if $\mathbf{B}_0$ and $\omega$ are then chosen appropriately.

Find the solution with $\mathbf{E}_0=E(1,i,0)$, where $E$ is real, and $\mathbf{k}=k(0,0,1)$. Compute the Poynting vector and state its physical significance.
```
