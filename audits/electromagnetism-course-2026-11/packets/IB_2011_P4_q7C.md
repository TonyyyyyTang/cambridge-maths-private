## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A plane electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has electric field
\[
\mathbf{E}=(E_0\sin k(z-ct),0,0).
\]
What are the wavevector, polarization vector and speed of the wave? Using Maxwell’s equations, find the magnetic field $\mathbf{B}$. Assuming the scalar potential vanishes, find a possible vector potential $\mathbf{A}$ for this wave, and verify that it gives the correct $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{B}$.
```
