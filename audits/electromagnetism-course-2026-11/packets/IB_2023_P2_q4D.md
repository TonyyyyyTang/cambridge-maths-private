## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Define what is meant by a \emph{capacitor} and by \emph{capacitance}.

Consider a cylindrical capacitor consisting of two concentric cylinders of length $L$, linear charge density $\lambda$ and radii $a$ and $b>a$, respectively. Assuming that $L\gg b$ and that end effects may be neglected, compute the electric field $E$ between the cylinders, the potential difference $V$, the capacitance $C$ and the energy $U$ stored in this system. Verify that $U=\frac12QV$ where $Q$ is the total charge.
```
