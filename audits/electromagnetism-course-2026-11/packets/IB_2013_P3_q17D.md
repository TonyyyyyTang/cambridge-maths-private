## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Three sides of a closed rectangular circuit $C$ are fixed and one is moving. The circuit lies in the plane $z=0$ and the sides are $x=0$, $y=0$, $x=a(t)$, $y=b$, where $a(t)$ is a given function of time. A magnetic field $\mathbf B=(0,0,\frac{\partial f}{\partial x})$ is applied, where $f(x,t)$ is a given function of $x$ and $t$ only. Find the magnetic flux $\Phi$ of $\mathbf B$ through the surface $S$ bounded by $C$.

Find an electric field $\mathbf E_0$ that satisfies the Maxwell equation
\[
\boldsymbol\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\frac{\partial\mathbf B}{\partial t}
\]
and then write down the most general solution $\mathbf E$ in terms of $\mathbf E_0$ and an undetermined scalar function independent of $f$.

Verify that
\[
\oint_C(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot d\mathbf r=-\frac{d\Phi}{dt},
\]
where $\mathbf v$ is the velocity of the relevant side of $C$. Interpret the left hand side of this equation.

If a unit current flows round $C$, what is the rate of work required to maintain the motion of the moving side of the rectangle? You should ignore any electromagnetic fields produced by the current.
```
