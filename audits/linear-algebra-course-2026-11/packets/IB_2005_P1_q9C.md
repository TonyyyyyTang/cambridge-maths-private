## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $V$ be a finite dimensional vector space over $\mathbf R$, and $V^*$ be the dual space of $V$. If $W$ is a subspace of $V$, we define the subspace $\alpha(W)$ of $V^*$ by
\[
\alpha(W)=\{f\in V^*:f(w)=0\text{ for all }w\text{ in }W\}.
\]

Prove that $\dim(\alpha(W))=\dim(V)-\dim(W)$. Deduce that, if $A=(a_{ij})$ is any real $m\times n$-matrix of rank $r$, the equations
\[
\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}x_j=0\quad(i=1,\ldots,m)
\]
have $n-r$ linearly independent solutions in $\mathbf R^n$.
```
