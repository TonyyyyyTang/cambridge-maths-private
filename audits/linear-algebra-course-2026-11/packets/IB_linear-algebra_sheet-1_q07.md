## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $\alpha:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ be the linear map given by
$\alpha:\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\ x_2\\ x_3\end{pmatrix}\mapsto
\begin{pmatrix}2&1&0\\0&2&1\\0&0&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\ x_2\\ x_3\end{pmatrix}$.
Find the matrix representing $\alpha$ relative to the basis
$\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}$, $\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}$, $\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}$
for both the domain and the range.

\textit{Write down} bases for the domain and range with respect to which the matrix of $\alpha$ is the identity.
```
