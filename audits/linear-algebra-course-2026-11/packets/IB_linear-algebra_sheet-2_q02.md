## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over the field $\mathbb F$; let $B$ be a basis for $V$, and let $P\in\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb F)$ be an invertible $n$-by-$n$ matrix over $\mathbb F$. Prove that there is are bases $C$ and $D$ for $V$ such that the change-of-basis matrices $[id_V]^C_B$ and $[id_V]^B_D$ are equal to $P$.
```
