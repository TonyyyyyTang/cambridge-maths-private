## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Show that right multiplication by $A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in M_{2\times2}(\C)$ defines a linear transformation $\rho_A:M_{2\times2}(\C)\to M_{2\times2}(\C)$. Find the matrix representing $\rho_A$ with respect to the basis
\[
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\]
of $M_{2\times2}(\C)$. Prove that the characteristic polynomial of $\rho_A$ is equal to the square of the characteristic polynomial of $A$, and that $A$ and $\rho_A$ have the same minimal polynomial.
```
