## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Define what is meant by the \emph{minimal polynomial} of a complex $n\times n$ matrix, and show that it is unique. Deduce that the minimal polynomial of a real $n\times n$ matrix has real coefficients.

For $n>2$, find an $n\times n$ matrix with minimal polynomial $(t-1)^2(t+1)$.
```
