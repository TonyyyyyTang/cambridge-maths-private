## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose that the vectors $\mathbf{e}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{e}_n$ form a basis for a real vector space $V$. Which of the following are also bases?

\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\mathbf{e}_1+\mathbf{e}_2,\mathbf{e}_2+\mathbf{e}_3,\ldots,\mathbf{e}_{n-1}+\mathbf{e}_n,\mathbf{e}_n$;
\item[(b)] $\mathbf{e}_1+\mathbf{e}_2,\mathbf{e}_2+\mathbf{e}_3,\ldots,\mathbf{e}_{n-1}+\mathbf{e}_n,\mathbf{e}_n+\mathbf{e}_1$;
\item[(c)] $\mathbf{e}_1-\mathbf{e}_n,\mathbf{e}_2+\mathbf{e}_{n-1},\ldots,\mathbf{e}_n+(-1)^n\mathbf{e}_1$.
\end{enumerate}
```
