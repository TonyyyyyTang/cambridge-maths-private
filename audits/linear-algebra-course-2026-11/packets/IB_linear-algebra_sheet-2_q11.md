## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Show that the dual of the space $P$ of real polynomials is isomorphic to the space $\R^{\N}$ of all sequences of real numbers, via the mapping which sends a linear form $\xi:P\to\R$ to the sequence $(\xi(1),\xi(t),\xi(t^2),\ldots)$.

In terms of this identification, describe the effect on a sequence $(a_0,a_1,a_2,\ldots)$ of the linear maps dual to each of the following linear maps $P\to P$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The map $D$ defined by $D(p)(t)=p'(t)$.
\item[(b)] The map $S$ defined by $S(p)(t)=p(t^2)$.
\item[(c)] The composite $DS$.
\item[(d)] The composite $SD$.
\end{enumerate}
Verify that $(DS)^*=S^*D^*$ and $(SD)^*=D^*S^*$.
```
