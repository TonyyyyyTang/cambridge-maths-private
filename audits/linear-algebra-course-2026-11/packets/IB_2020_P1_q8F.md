## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $\mathcal M_n$ denote the vector space of $n\times n$ matrices over a field $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. What is the \emph{rank} $r(A)$ of a matrix $A\in\mathcal M_n$?

Show, stating accurately any preliminary results that you require, that $r(A)=n$ if and only if $A$ is non-singular, i.e. $\det A\neq 0$.

Does $\mathcal M_n$ have a basis consisting of non-singular matrices? Justify your answer.

Suppose that an $n\times n$ matrix $A$ is non-singular and every entry of $A$ is either $0$ or $1$. Let $c_n$ be the largest possible number of $1$'s in such an $A$. Show that $c_n\leq n^2-n+1$. Is this bound attained? Justify your answer.

[\emph{Standard properties of the adjugate matrix can be assumed, if accurately stated.}]
```
