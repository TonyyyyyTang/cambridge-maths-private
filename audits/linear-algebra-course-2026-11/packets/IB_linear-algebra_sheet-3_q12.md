## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $C$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over $\C$, and write $C=A+iB$, where $A$ and $B$ are real $n\times n$ matrices. By considering $\det(A+\lambda B)$ as a function of $\lambda$, show that if $C$ is invertible then there exists a real number $\lambda$ such that $A+\lambda B$ is invertible. Deduce that if two $n\times n$ real matrices $P$ and $Q$ are similar when regarded as matrices over $\C$, then they are similar as matrices over $\R$.
```
