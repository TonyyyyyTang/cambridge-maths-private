## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
If $q$ is a quadratic form on a finite-dimensional real vector space $V$, what is the associated \emph{symmetric bilinear form} $\varphi(\cdot,\cdot)$? Prove that there is a basis for $V$ with respect to which the matrix for $\varphi$ is diagonal. What is the \emph{signature} of $q$?

If $R\leq V$ is a subspace such that $\varphi(r,v)=0$ for all $r\in R$ and all $v\in V$, show that $q'(v+R)=q(v)$ defines a quadratic form on the quotient vector space $V/R$. Show that the signature of $q'$ is the same as that of $q$.

If $e,f\in V$ are vectors such that $\varphi(e,e)=0$ and $\varphi(e,f)=1$, show that there is a direct sum decomposition $V=\operatorname{span}(e,f)\oplus U$ such that the signature of $q|_U$ is the same as that of $q$.
```
