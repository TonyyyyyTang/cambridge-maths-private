## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $V$ be a real vector space. Define the \emph{dual} vector space $V^*$ of $V$. If $U$ is a subspace of $V$, define the \emph{annihilator} $U^0$ of $U$. If $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ is a basis for $V$, define its dual $x_1^*,x_2^*,\ldots,x_n^*$ and prove that it is a basis for $V^*$.

If $V$ has basis $x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4$ and $U$ is the subspace spanned by
\[
x_1+2x_2+3x_3+4x_4\quad\text{and}\quad 5x_1+6x_2+7x_3+8x_4,
\]
give a basis for $U^0$ in terms of the dual basis $x_1^*,x_2^*,x_3^*,x_4^*$.
```
