## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Let $\alpha:V\to V$ be an endomorphism of a finite dimensional vector space $V$. Show that
\[
V\ge\operatorname{im}(\alpha)\ge\operatorname{im}(\alpha^2)\ge\ldots
\quad\text{and}\quad
\{0\}\le\operatorname{Ker}(\alpha)\le\operatorname{Ker}(\alpha^2)\le\ldots.
\]
If $r_k=\operatorname{rk}(\alpha^k)$, deduce that $r_k\ge r_{k+1}$ and that $r_k-r_{k+1}\ge r_{k+1}-r_{k+2}$. Conclude that if, for some $k\ge0$, we have $r_k=r_{k+1}$, then $r_k=r_{k+\ell}$ for all $\ell\ge0$.
\item[(ii)] Suppose that $\dim(V)=5$, $\alpha^3=0$, but $\alpha^2\ne0$. What possibilities are there for $\operatorname{rk}(\alpha)$ and $\operatorname{rk}(\alpha^2)$?
\end{enumerate}
```
