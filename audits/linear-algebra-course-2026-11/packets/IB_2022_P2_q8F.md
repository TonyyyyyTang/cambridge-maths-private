## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $V$ be a real vector space (not necessarily finite-dimensional). Define the \emph{dual space} $V^*$. Prove that if $f_1,f_2\in V^*$ are such that $f_1(v)f_2(v)=0$ for all $v\in V$, then $f_1$ or $f_2$ is the zero element in $V^*$.

Now suppose that $V$ is a finite-dimensional real vector space.

Let $\phi$ be a symmetric bilinear form on $V$. State Sylvester’s law of inertia for $\phi$.

Let $q$ be a quadratic form on $V$, let $r$ denote its rank and $\sigma$ its signature. Show that $q$ can be factorised as $q(v)=f_1(v)f_2(v)$ with $f_1,f_2\in V^*$ for all $v\in V$ if and only if $r+|\sigma|\leq2$.

A vector $v_0\in V$ is called isotropic if $q(v_0)=0$. Show that if there exist $v_1$ and $v_2$ in $V$ such that $q(v_1)>0$ and $q(v_2)<0$, then one can construct a basis of $V$ consisting of isotropic vectors.
```
