## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Define a \emph{quadratic form} on a finite dimensional real vector space. What does it mean for a quadratic form to be \emph{positive definite}?

Find a basis with respect to which the quadratic form
\[
x^2+2xy+2y^2+2yz+3z^2
\]
is diagonal. Is this quadratic form positive definite?
```
