## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
For which real numbers $x$ do the vectors
\[
(x,1,1,1),\quad(1,x,1,1),\quad(1,1,x,1),\quad(1,1,1,x),
\]
\emph{not} form a basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$? For each such value of $x$, what is the dimension of the subspace of $\mathbb{R}^4$ that they span? For each such value of $x$, provide a basis for the spanned subspace, and extend this basis to a basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$.
```
