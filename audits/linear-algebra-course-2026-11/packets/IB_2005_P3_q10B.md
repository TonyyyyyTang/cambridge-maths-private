## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $S$ be the vector space of functions $f:\mathbf R\to\mathbf R$ such that the $n$th derivative of $f$ is defined and continuous for every $n\geqslant0$. Define linear maps $A,B:S\to S$ by $A(f)=df/dx$ and $B(f)(x)=xf(x)$. Show that
\[
[A,B]=1_S,
\]
where in this question $[A,B]$ means $AB-BA$ and $1_S$ is the identity map on $S$.

Now let $V$ be any real vector space with linear maps $A,B:V\to V$ such that $[A,B]=1_V$. Suppose that there is a nonzero element $y\in V$ with $Ay=0$. Let $W$ be the subspace of $V$ spanned by $y$, $By$, $B^2y$, and so on. Show that $A(By)$ is in $W$ and give a formula for it. More generally, show that $A(B^iy)$ is in $W$ for each $i\geqslant0$, and give a formula for it.

Show, using your formula or otherwise, that $\{y,By,B^2y,\ldots\}$ are linearly independent. (Or, equivalently: show that $y,By,B^2y,\ldots,B^ny$ are linearly independent for every $n\geqslant0$.)
```
