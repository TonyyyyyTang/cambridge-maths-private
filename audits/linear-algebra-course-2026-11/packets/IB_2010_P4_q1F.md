## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Define the notion of an inner product on a finite-dimensional real vector space $V$, and the notion of a self-adjoint linear map $\alpha:V\to V$.

Suppose that $V$ is the space of real polynomials of degree at most $n$ in a variable $t$. Show that
\[
\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{-1}^1f(t)g(t)\,dt
\]
is an inner product on $V$, and that the map $\alpha:V\to V$:
\[
\alpha(f)(t)=(1-t^2)f''(t)-2tf'(t)
\]
is self-adjoint.
```
