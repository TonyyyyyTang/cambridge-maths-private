## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $V$ denote the vector space of polynomials $f(x,y)$ in two variables of total degree at most $n$. Find the dimension of $V$.

If $S:V\to V$ is defined by
\[(Sf)(x,y)=x^2\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x^2}+y^2\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial y^2},\]
find the kernel of $S$ and the image of $S$. Compute the trace of $S$ for each $n$ with $1\leqslant n\leqslant4$.
```
