## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Determine for which values of $x\in\C$ the matrix
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}x&1&1\\1-x&0&-1\\2&2x&1\end{pmatrix}
\]
is invertible. Determine the rank of $M$ as a function of $x$. Find the adjugate and hence the inverse of $M$ for general $x$.
```
