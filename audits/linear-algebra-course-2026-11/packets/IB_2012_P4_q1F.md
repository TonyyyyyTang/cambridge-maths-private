## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $V$ be a complex vector space with basis $\{e_1,\ldots,e_n\}$. Define $T:V\to V$ by $T(e_i)=e_i-e_{i+1}$ for $i<n$ and $T(e_n)=e_n-e_1$. Show that $T$ is diagonalizable and find its eigenvalues. [You may use any theorems you wish, as long as you state them clearly.]
```
