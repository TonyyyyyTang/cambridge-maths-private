## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over a field $k$. Describe a bijective correspondence between the set of bilinear forms on $V$, and the set of linear maps of $V$ to its dual space $V^*$. If $\phi_1,\phi_2$ are non-degenerate bilinear forms on $V$, prove that there exists an isomorphism $\alpha:V\to V$ such that $\phi_2(u,v)=\phi_1(u,\alpha v)$ for all $u,v\in V$. If furthermore both $\phi_1,\phi_2$ are symmetric, show that $\alpha$ is self-adjoint (i.e. equals its adjoint) with respect to $\phi_1$.
```
