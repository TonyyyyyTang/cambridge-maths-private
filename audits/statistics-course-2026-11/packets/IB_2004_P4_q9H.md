## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose that $Y_1,\ldots,Y_n$ are independent random variables, with $Y_i$ having the normal distribution with mean $\beta x_i$ and variance $\sigma^2$; here $\beta$, $\sigma^2$ are unknown and $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ are known constants.

Derive the least-squares estimate of $\beta$.

Explain carefully how to test the hypothesis $H_0:\beta=0$ against $H_1:\beta\ne0$.
```
