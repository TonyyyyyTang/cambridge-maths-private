## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
(a) Define a \emph{uniformly most powerful (UMP) test} when $X\sim f(\cdot|\theta)$ for $\theta\in\Theta$, and the two hypotheses correspond to
\[
\begin{aligned}
H_0 &: \theta\in\Theta_0\subseteq\Theta\\
H_1 &: \theta\in\Theta_1\subseteq\Theta.
\end{aligned}
\]
(b) Let $f(x|\theta)$ be the logistic location probability density function
\[
f(x|\theta)=\frac{e^{(x-\theta)}}{(1+e^{(x-\theta)})^2},\quad-\infty<x<\infty,\quad-\infty<\theta<\infty.
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Based on one observation $X$, find the most powerful size-$\alpha$ test of $H_0:\theta=0$ versus $H_1:\theta=1$. You may use any results from the lectures without proof provided you state them clearly.
\item[(ii)] Prove that the test in part (i) is UMP of size $\alpha$ for testing $H_0:\theta\leq0$ versus $H_1:\theta>0$.
\end{enumerate}
```
