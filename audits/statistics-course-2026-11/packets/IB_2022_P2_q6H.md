## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose that $X_1,X_2,\ldots,X_n$ are i.i.d. random variables with probability density function
\[
f_\theta(x)=\frac12+\frac{1_{\{x<\theta\}}}{2\theta}\quad\text{for }x\in[0,1],
\]
with parameter $\theta\in(0,1)$.

(a) Write down the likelihood function, and show that the maximum likelihood estimator coincides with one of the samples.

(b) Consider the estimator $\tilde\theta=4\overline X-1$ where $\overline X=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i$. Is $\tilde\theta$ unbiased? Construct an asymptotic $(1-\alpha)$-confidence interval for $\theta$ around this estimator.
```
