## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose that $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are independent normal random variables of unknown mean $\theta$ and variance 1. It is desired to test the hypothesis $H_0:\theta\le0$ against the alternative $H_1:\theta>0$. Show that there is a uniformly most powerful test of size $\alpha=1/20$ and identify a critical region for such a test in the case $n=9$. If you appeal to any theoretical result from the course you should also prove it.

[\emph{The 95th percentile of the standard normal distribution is 1.65.}]
```
