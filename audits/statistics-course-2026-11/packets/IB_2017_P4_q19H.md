## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
(a) State and prove the Neyman–Pearson lemma.

(b) Let $X$ be a real random variable with density $f(x)=(2\theta x+1-\theta)1_{[0,1]}(x)$ with $-1\leq\theta\leq1$.

Find a most powerful test of size $\alpha$ of $H_0:\theta=0$ versus $H_1:\theta=1$.

Find a uniformly most powerful test of size $\alpha$ of $H_0:\theta=0$ versus $H_1:\theta>0$.
```
