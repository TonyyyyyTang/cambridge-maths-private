## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose the \textbf{single} random variable $X$ has a uniform distribution on the interval $[0,\theta]$ and it is required to estimate $\theta$ with the loss function
\[
L(\theta,a)=c(\theta-a)^2,
\]
where $c>0$.

Find the posterior distribution for $\theta$ and the optimal Bayes point estimate with respect to the prior distribution with density $p(\theta)=\theta e^{-\theta},\ \theta>0$.
```
