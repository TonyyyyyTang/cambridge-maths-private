## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose that $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are i.i.d. $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ random variables.

(a) Compute the MLEs $\widehat{\mu}$, $\widehat{\sigma}^2$ for the unknown parameters $\mu$, $\sigma^2$.

(b) Give the definition of an \emph{unbiased estimator}. Determine whether $\widehat{\mu}$, $\widehat{\sigma}^2$ are unbiased estimators for $\mu$, $\sigma^2$.
```
