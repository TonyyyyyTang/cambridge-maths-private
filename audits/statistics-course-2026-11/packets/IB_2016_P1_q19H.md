## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
(a) What does it mean to say a statistic $T$ is \emph{sufficient} for an unknown parameter $\theta$? State the factorisation criterion for sufficiency and prove it in the discrete case.

(b) State and prove the Rao-Blackwell theorem.

(c) Let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be independent samples from the uniform distribution on $[-\theta,\theta]$ for an unknown positive parameter $\theta$. Consider the two-dimensional statistic
\[
T=(\min_iX_i,\max_iX_i).
\]
Prove that $T$ is sufficient for $\theta$. Determine, with proof, whether or not $T$ is minimally sufficient.
```
