## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Consider the normal linear model $Y=X\beta+\varepsilon$ where $X$ is a known matrix of predictors, $\beta\in\R^p$ is an unknown vector of parameters, and $\varepsilon\sim N_n(0,\sigma^2I)$ is a vector of normal errors with $\sigma>0$. Let $X=(X_0,X_1)$, where $X_0\in\R^{n\times p_0}$ and $X_1\in\R^{n\times p_1}$ with $p_0+p_1=p$, and correspondingly write $\beta^\top=(\beta_0^\top,\beta_1^\top)$ where $\beta_0\in\R^{p_0}$ and $\beta_1\in\R^{p_1}$. We wish to test the null hypothesis $H_0$ that $\beta_1=0$ against the alternative that $\beta_1\ne0$. We assume throughout that $X_0$ has full column rank.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Write down the maximum likelihood estimator $(\widehat\beta_0,\widehat\sigma^2)$ of $(\beta_0,\sigma^2)$ in the model given by the null hypothesis $H_0$.
\item[(ii)] Assuming that $X$ has full column rank, write down the $F$-test statistic for testing $H_0$.
\item[(iii)] Let $R=Y-X_0\widehat\beta_0$ be the residuals from fitting the model given by $H_0$. Show that under the null, the distribution of $R/\sigma$ is the same as that of $(I-P_0)Z$ where $Z\sim N_n(0,I)$ and $P_0\in\R^{n\times n}$ is a matrix you should specify.
\item[(iv)] Let $X_{1j}\in\R^n$ be the $j$th column of the matrix $X_1$ and suppose $\|X_{1j}\|=\sqrt n$ for all $j$. It is known that if $\zeta\sim N(0,1)$, then $\mathbb P(|\zeta|>t)\le e^{-t^2/2}$ for $t\ge0$. Show that for each $j$,
\[
\mathbb P\left(\frac1{\sqrt n}\left|X_{1j}^\top(I-P_0)Z\right|>t\right)
\le e^{-t^2/2}.
\]
\item[(v)] Let $X_{1j}\in\R^n$ be the $j$th column of the matrix $X_1$. We do not assume that $X$ has full column rank, but suppose that $\sigma$ is known. Consider the test statistic
\[
T=\frac1{\sigma\sqrt n}\max_{j=1,\ldots,p_1}|X_{1j}^\top R|.
\]
Let $\alpha\in(0,1)$. Show that the test that rejects $H_0$ when $T\ge\sqrt{2\log(p_1/\alpha)}$ has size at most $\alpha$.

[\emph{Hint: Given events $A_1,\ldots,A_{p_1}$, a union bound yields $\mathbb P(\bigcup_{j=1}^{p_1}A_j)\le\sum_{j=1}^{p_1}\mathbb P(A_j)$.}]
\item[(vi)] Now consider the case where $\sigma>0$ is unknown (and $X$ does not necessarily have full column rank). Find, with justification, a test statistic for the null $H_0$ against the alternative $\beta_1\ne0$ that under the null has the same distribution as
\[
\max_{j=1,\ldots,p_1}
\frac{|X_{1j}^\top(I-P_0)Z|}{\|(I-P_0)Z\|}.
\]
\end{enumerate}
```
