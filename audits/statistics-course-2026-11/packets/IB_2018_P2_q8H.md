## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Define a \emph{simple hypothesis}. Define the terms \emph{size} and \emph{power} for a test of one simple hypothesis against another. State the Neyman-Pearson lemma.

There is a single observation of a random variable $X$ which has a probability density function $f(x)$. Construct a best test of size $0.05$ for the null hypothesis
\[
H_0:\quad f(x)=\tfrac12,\qquad -1\leq x\leq 1,
\]
against the alternative hypothesis
\[
H_1:\quad f(x)=\frac34(1-x^2),\qquad -1\leq x\leq 1.
\]
Calculate the power of your test.
```
