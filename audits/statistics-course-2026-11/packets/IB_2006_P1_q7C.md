## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
A random sample $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ is taken from a normal distribution having unknown mean $\theta$ and variance $1$. Find the maximum likelihood estimate $\hat\theta_M$ for $\theta$ based on $X_1,\ldots,X_n$.

Suppose that we now take a Bayesian point of view and regard $\theta$ itself as a normal random variable of known mean $\mu$ and variance $\tau^{-1}$. Find the Bayes’ estimate $\hat\theta_B$ for $\theta$ based on $X_1,\ldots,X_n$, corresponding to the quadratic loss function $(\theta-a)^2$.
```
