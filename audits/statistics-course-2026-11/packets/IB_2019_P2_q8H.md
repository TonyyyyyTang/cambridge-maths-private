## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose that $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are i.i.d. coin tosses with probability $\theta$ of obtaining a head.

(a) Compute the posterior distribution of $\theta$ given the observations $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ in the case of a uniform prior on $[0,1]$.

(b) Give the definition of the \emph{quadratic error loss function}.

(c) Determine the value $\widehat\theta$ of $\theta$ which minimizes the quadratic error loss function. Justify your answer. Calculate $\mathbb{E}[\widehat\theta]$.

[\emph{You may use that the $\beta(a,b)$, $a,b>0$, distribution has density function on $[0,1]$ given by}
\[
c_{a,b}\,x^{a-1}(1-x)^{b-1}
\]
\emph{where $c_{a,b}$ is a normalizing constant. You may also use without proof that the mean of a $\beta(a,b)$ random variable is $a/(a+b)$.}]
```
