## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Find a countable subset $X$ of $\mathbb R$ that is \emph{dense}, that is meets every open subset of $\mathbb R$. Using uniform continuity or otherwise find a countable dense subset of $C([0,1])$ with the sup norm $\|\cdot\|_\infty$. Does there exist a countable dense subset of $l_\infty$, the space of bounded real sequences with the sup norm?
```
