## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $E$ be a subset of $\mathbb R^n$. Prove that the following conditions on $E$ are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $E$ is closed and bounded.
\item[(ii)] $E$ has the Bolzano–Weierstrass property (i.e., every sequence in $E$ has a subsequence convergent to a point of $E$).
\item[(iii)] Every continuous real-valued function on $E$ is bounded.
\end{enumerate}
[\emph{The Bolzano–Weierstrass property for bounded closed intervals in $\mathbb R^1$ may be assumed}.]
```
