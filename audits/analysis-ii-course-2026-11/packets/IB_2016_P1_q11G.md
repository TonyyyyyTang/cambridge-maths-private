## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $(X,d)$ be a metric space.

(a) What does it mean to say that $(x_n)_n$ is a \emph{Cauchy sequence} in $X$? Show that if $(x_n)_n$ is a Cauchy sequence, then it converges if it contains a convergent subsequence.

(b) Let $(x_n)_n$ be a Cauchy sequence in $X$.

(i) Show that for every $m\geq1$, the sequence $(d(x_m,x_n))_n$ converges to some $d_m\in\mathbb{R}$.

(ii) Show that $d_m\to0$ as $m\to\infty$.

(iii) Let $(y_n)_n$ be a subsequence of $(x_n)_n$. If $\ell,m$ are such that $y_\ell=x_m$, show that $d(y_\ell,y_n)\to d_m$ as $n\to\infty$.

(iv) Show also that for every $m$ and $n$,
\[
d_m-d_n\leq d(x_m,x_n)\leq d_m+d_n.
\]

(v) Deduce that $(x_n)_n$ has a subsequence $(y_n)_n$ such that for every $m$ and $n$,
\[
d(y_{m+1},y_m)\leq\frac13d(y_m,y_{m-1})
\]
and
\[
d(y_{m+1},y_{n+1})\leq\frac12d(y_m,y_n).
\]

(c) Suppose that every closed subset $Y$ of $X$ has the property that every contraction mapping $Y\to Y$ has a fixed point. Prove that $X$ is complete.
```
