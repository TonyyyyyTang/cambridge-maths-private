## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Which of the following functions $f:[0,\infty)\to\R$ are (a) uniformly continuous; (b) bounded?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $f(x)=\sin x^2$;
\item[(ii)] $f(x)=\inf\{|x-n^2|:n\in\N\}$;
\item[(iii)] $f(x)=(\sin x^3)/(x+1)$.
\end{enumerate}
```
