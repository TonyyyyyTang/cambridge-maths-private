## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of real-valued functions on $[0,1]$ converging uniformly to a function $f$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] If $\mathcal D_n$ is the set of discontinuities of $f_n$ and $\mathcal D$ is the set of discontinuities of $f$, show that $\mathcal D\subseteq\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty\bigcap_{j=n}^\infty\mathcal D_j$.
\item[(b)] Suppose that for some finite $k$, each $f_n$ is discontinuous at most at $k$ points. What can you say about the set of discontinuities of $f$?
\end{enumerate}
```
