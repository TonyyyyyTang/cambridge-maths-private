## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Define what is meant by a \emph{uniformly continuous} function $f$ on a subset $E$ of a metric space. Show that every continuous function on a closed, bounded interval is uniformly continuous. [You may assume the Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem.]

Suppose that a function $g:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb{R}$ is continuous and tends to a finite limit at $\infty$. Is $g$ necessarily uniformly continuous on $[0,\infty)$? Give a proof or a counterexample as appropriate.
```
