## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
What does it mean for a function $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ of several variables to be differentiable at a point $\mathbf x$? State and prove the chain rule for functions of several variables. For each of the following two functions from $\mathbb R^2$ to $\mathbb R$, give with proof the set of points at which it is differentiable:
\[g_1(x,y)=\biggl\{\begin{aligned}&(x^2-y^2)\sin\tfrac1{x^2-y^2}&&\quad \text{if }x\neq\pm y\\&0&&\quad \text{otherwise;}\end{aligned}\]
\[g_2(x,y)=\biggl\{\begin{aligned}&(x^2+y^2)\sin\tfrac1{x^2+y^2}&&\quad \text{if at least one of }x,y\text{ is not }0\\&0&&\quad \text{if }x=y=0.\end{aligned}\]
```
