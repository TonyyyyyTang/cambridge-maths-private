Which of the following functions $f:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ are (a) uniformly continuous; (b) bounded?

(i)$f(x)=\sin x^2$; $\qquad$ (ii) $f(x)=\inf\{|x-n^2|:n\in\mathbb N\}$; $\qquad$ (iii) $f(x)=(\sin x^3)/(x+1)$.
