## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Define a \emph{contraction mapping} between two metric spaces. State and prove the contraction mapping theorem. Use this to show that the equation $x=\cos x$ has a unique real solution.

State the mean value inequality. Let $f:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ be the map given by
\[
f(x,y)=\left(\frac{\cos x+\cos y-1}{2},\cos x-\cos y\right).
\]
Prove that $f$ has a fixed point. [\emph{Hint: Find a suitable subset of $\mathbb R^2$ on which $f$ is a contraction mapping.}]
```
