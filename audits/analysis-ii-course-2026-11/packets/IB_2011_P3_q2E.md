## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose $f$ is a uniformly continuous mapping from a metric space $X$ to a metric space $Y$. Prove that $f(x_n)$ is a Cauchy sequence in $Y$ for every Cauchy sequence $x_n$ in $X$.

Let $f$ be a continuous mapping between metric spaces and suppose that $f$ has the property that $f(x_n)$ is a Cauchy sequence whenever $x_n$ is a Cauchy sequence. Is it true that $f$ must be uniformly continuous? Justify your answer.
```
