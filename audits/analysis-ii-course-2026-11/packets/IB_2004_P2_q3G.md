## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Consider a sequence of continuous functions $F_n:[-1,1]\to\mathbb R$. Suppose that the functions $F_n$ converge uniformly to some continuous function $F$. Show that the integrals $\int_{-1}^1 F_n(x)dx$ converge to $\int_{-1}^1 F(x)dx$.

Give an example to show that, even if the functions $F_n(x)$ and $F(x)$ are differentiable, the derivatives $F'_n(0)$ need not converge to $F'(0)$.
```
