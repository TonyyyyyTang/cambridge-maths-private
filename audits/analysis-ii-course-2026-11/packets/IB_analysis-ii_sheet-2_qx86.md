## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Suppose that $g:[0,1]\times[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ is a continuous function. Show that one can define a map $G:C[0,1]\to C[0,1]$ by setting
\[
(Gf)(x)=\int_0^1g(x,t)f(t)dt
\]
for $f\in C[0,1]$.

Show further that $G:C[0,1]\to C[0,1]$ is a linear map, and that $G$ is a continuous map with respect to the uniform (sup) norm. Is it continuous with respect to the $L^1$ norm? (Can you justify your answer? If not at least have a guess!)
```
