## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $E$ be a subset of $\mathbb R^n$ which is both open and closed. Show that $E$ is either the whole of $\mathbb R^n$ or the empty set. [Method: suppose for a contradiction that $x\in E$ but $y\in\mathbb R^n\setminus E$. Define a function $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ by setting $f(t)=1$ if the point $tx+(1-t)y$ belongs to $E$, and $f(t)=0$ otherwise; now recall a suitable theorem from Analysis I.]
```
