## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $X$ and $X'$ be metric spaces with metrics $d$ and $d'$. If $u=(x,x')$ and $v=(y,y')$ are any two points of $X\times X'$, prove that the formula
\[
D(u,v)=\max\bigl\{d(x,y),d'(x',y')\bigr\}
\]
defines a metric on $X\times X'$. If $X=X'$, prove that the diagonal $\Delta$ of $X\times X$ is closed in $X\times X$.
```
