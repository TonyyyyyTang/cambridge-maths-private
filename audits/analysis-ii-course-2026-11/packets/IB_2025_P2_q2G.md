## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
For each of the following sequences of functions $f_n:\R\to\R$, determine whether $(f_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ converges uniformly, justifying your answer:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[i)] $f_n(x)=\tanh(nx)$,
\item[ii)] $f_n(x)=\sin\left(x+\frac1n\right)$,
\item[iii)] $f_n(x)=\dfrac{\exp\left(x+\frac1n\right)}{\cosh(ax)}$ (your answer may depend on the value of $a\in\R$),
\item[iv)] $f_n(x)=2n\left[h\left(x+\frac1n\right)-h\left(x-\frac1n\right)\right]$,

where $h:\R\to\R$ is differentiable with $h'$ uniformly continuous.
\end{enumerate}
```
