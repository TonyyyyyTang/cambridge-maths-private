## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R^n$ be a function where $U$ is an open subset of $\mathbb R^m$, and let $a\in U$. Define what it means that \emph{$f$ is differentiable at $a$} and define \emph{the derivative of $f$ at $a$}. Define what it means that \emph{$f$ is continuously differentiable at $a$}. Show that a linear map $\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R^n$ is continuously differentiable at every point of $\mathbb R^m$.

State and prove the mean value inequality. Let $U$ be an open, connected subset of $\mathbb R^m$. Let $f:U\to\mathbb R^n$ be a differentiable function such that $Df|_a$ is the zero map for all $a\in U$. Show that $f$ is a constant function.

State the inverse function theorem. Consider the curve $C$ in $\mathbb R^2$ defined by the equation
\[
x^2+y+\cos(xy)=1.
\]
Show that there exist an open neighbourhood $U$ of $(0,0)$ in $\mathbb R^2$, an open interval $I$ in $\mathbb R$ containing $0$ and a continuous function $g:I\to\mathbb R$ such that $U\cap C$ is the graph of $g$, \emph{i.e.},
\[
\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x\in I,\ y=g(x)\}=U\cap C.
\]
```
