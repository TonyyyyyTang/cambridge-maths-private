## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Let $A\subset\mathbb{R}$. What does it mean to say that a sequence of real-valued functions on $A$ is \emph{uniformly convergent}?

(i) If a sequence $(f_n)$ of real-valued functions on $A$ converges uniformly to $f$, and each $f_n$ is continuous, must $f$ also be continuous?

(ii) Let $f_n(x)=e^{-nx}$. Does the sequence $(f_n)$ converge uniformly on $[0,1]$?

(iii) If a sequence $(f_n)$ of real-valued functions on $[-1,1]$ converges uniformly to $f$, and each $f_n$ is differentiable, must $f$ also be differentiable?

Give a proof or counterexample in each case.
```
