## Question (verbatim question.tex)
```latex
Which of the following sets are a) open? b) closed?

(i) $\{0\}$ in an arbitrary normed space $X$,

(ii) The whole space $X$ in an arbitrary normed space $X$,

(iii) $\{x:\text{there exists N such that }x_n=0\text{ for all }n>N\}$ in $l_\infty$,

(iv) $\{x:x_n\to0\}$ in $l_\infty$,

(v) $\{f:f(1/2)=0\}$ in $C([0,1])$ with the sup norm $\|\cdot\|_\infty$,

(vi) $\{f:\int_0^1 f=0\}$ in $C([0,1])$ with the sup norm $\|\cdot\|_\infty$,

(vii) $\{f:f(1/2)=0\}$ in $C([0,1])$ with the norm $\|\cdot\|_1$ defined in Q2,

(viii) $\{f:\int_0^1 f=0\}$ in $C([0,1])$ with the norm $\|\cdot\|_1$.
```
